Part V · Lesson 25

Large cardinals III: from Woodin to rank-into-rank, and determinacy大基数（三）：从伍丁到秩入秩，以及决定性

In Lesson 24 a measurable cardinal became an elementary embedding j : V → M. This lesson pushes that one picture as far as it can go, makes M look more and more like V, and watches it hit a wall at M = V. Then comes the payoff: infinite games. Large cardinals turn out to be exactly what is needed to decide who wins definable games on the reals. 第 24 课里，可测基数化身为初等嵌入 j : V → M。本课把这一幅图推到极致：让 M 越来越像 V，直到在 M = V 处撞上一堵墙。随后是回报：无穷博弈。原来，要判定实数上可定义博弈的胜负，所需要的恰恰就是大基数。

§1

The embedding paradigm: how close can M get to V?嵌入范式：M 能离 V 多近？

Prerequisites前置: L14 · the hierarchy V累积层级 V L24 · j : V → M, critical pointj : V → M 与临界点
One idea. Almost every large cardinal above a measurable is “there is j : V → M with critical point κ, and M contains a lot of V”. The more of V that M is required to contain, the stronger the axiom. 核心思想。可测基数之上的大基数几乎都形如“存在临界点为 κ 的 j : V → M，并且 M 包含 V 的很大一部分”。要求 M 包含的 V 越多，公理就越强。
Setting (from L24). M is a transitive class containing all ordinals, j : V → M is elementary, and the critical point crit(j) = κ is the least ordinal with j(κ) ≠ κ; then j(κ) > κ and κ is measurable. Notation: λM ⊆ M means every function λ → M (in V) already belongs to M (“M is closed under λ-sequences”). 设定（承接第 24 课）。M 是包含全体序数的传递类，j : V → M 是初等嵌入，临界点 crit(j) = κ 是使 j(κ) ≠ κ 的最小序数；此时 j(κ) > κ，且 κ 是可测基数。记号：λM ⊆ M 表示（V 中的）每个函数 λ → M 都已属于 M（“M 对 λ-序列封闭”）。

Drag the slider. The big V-shape is the universe (rank grows upward). The blue region is M. Also, the slider shows one embedding, while “strong” and “supercompact” demand embeddings for every λ. That is why a huge cardinal (one very closed embedding) need not be supercompact, even though “huge” has higher consistency strength. ⚠ 常见误区。此图只是示意。“M 含有多少 V”并不是一个数：对 λ-序列封闭（超紧）和包含 Vλ（强）是两类不同的要求。另外，滑块展示的是一个嵌入，而“强”和“超紧”要求对每个 λ 都有嵌入。所以巨大基数（只要一个封闭性极强的嵌入）不一定是超紧的，尽管“巨大”的一致性强度更高。
§2

Strong cardinals: M contains V up to rank λ强基数：M 包含直到秩 λ 的 V

Prerequisites前置: §1 L24 · ultrapower Ult(V, U)超幂 Ult(V, U)
One idea. The cleanest way to make M “close to V” is to demand that M contains an initial segment Vλ of the universe. A measurable already gives λ = κ+1, and not one level more for free. 核心思想。让 M“接近 V”最干净的办法，是要求 M 包含宇宙的一个初始段 Vλ。可测基数已经给出 λ = κ+1，但再多一层就不是白给的了。
Definition. κ is λ-strong if there is an elementary j : V → M, M transitive, with crit(j) = κ, j(κ) > λ and Vλ ⊆ M. κ is strong if it is λ-strong for every ordinal λ.
Fact ZFC ⊢: κ is (κ+1)-strong ⇔ κ is measurable. 定义. 若存在初等嵌入 j : V → M（M 传递），使 crit(j) = κ、j(κ) > λ 且 Vλ ⊆ M，则称 κ 为 λ-强基数。若对每个序数 λ，κ 都是 λ-强的，则称 κ 为强基数。
事实 ZFC ⊢：κ 是 (κ+1)-强的 ⇔ κ 是可测基数。

Move λ up the rank ruler. Watch three kinds of sets: a subset x ⊆ κ (rank ≤ κ, so in Vκ+1), a normal measure U ⊆ P(κ) (rank κ+1, so in Vκ+2 but not Vκ+1), and an arbitrary W ⊆ P(P(κ)) (rank ≤ κ+2). 沿着秩的标尺移动 λ。留意三类集合：子集 x ⊆ κ（秩 ≤ κ，故属于 Vκ+1）、正规测度 U ⊆ P(κ)（秩为 κ+1，故属于 Vκ+2 而不属于 Vκ+1），以及任意的 W ⊆ P(P(κ))（秩 ≤ κ+2）。

Also: strong cardinals are relatively weak in consistency strength (below Woodin), even though “strong” sounds strong. ⚠ 常见误区。“Vλ ⊆ M”并不表示 j 在 Vλ 上是恒等映射：j 照样移动 κ。它只是说秩 < λ 的集合都在 M 里。另外，强基数的一致性强度其实不算高（低于伍丁基数），别被名字骗了。
§3

Woodin cardinals: strength spread out below δ伍丁基数：分布在 δ 之下的强度

Prerequisites前置: §2 · λ-strongλ-强 L23 · Mahlo, weakly compact马洛、弱紧
One idea. A Woodin cardinal δ need not itself be a critical point (the least one is not). Its strength lives in the cardinals below it: for every way f of “growing” inside δ, some κ < δ closed under f is strong enough to reach past j(f)(κ). 核心思想。伍丁基数 δ 本身不必是临界点（最小的伍丁基数就不是）。它的强度在它下方的基数里：对 δ 内部每一种“增长方式” f，都有某个对 f 封闭的 κ < δ，其强度足以覆盖到 j(f)(κ)。
Definition (Woodin). A cardinal δ is Woodin if for every function f : δ → δ there is κ < δ with f″κ ⊆ κ (κ is closed under f) and an elementary j : V → M, M transitive, with crit(j) = κ and Vj(f)(κ) ⊆ M.
Equivalent form. δ is Woodin ⇔ δ is inaccessible and for every A ⊆ Vδ some κ < δ is <δ-A-strong: for every α < δ there is j : V → M with crit(j) = κ, j(κ) > α, Vα ⊆ M and j(A) ∩ Vα = A ∩ Vα. 定义（伍丁）. 称基数 δ 为伍丁基数，若对每个函数 f : δ → δ，都存在 κ < δ 满足 f″κ ⊆ κ（κ 对 f 封闭），以及初等嵌入 j : V → M（M 传递），使 crit(j) = κ 且 Vj(f)(κ) ⊆ M。
等价形式. δ 是伍丁基数 ⇔ δ 不可达，并且对每个 A ⊆ Vδ，都有某个 κ < δ 是 <δ-A-强的：对每个 α < δ，存在 j : V → M，使 crit(j) = κ、j(κ) > α、Vα ⊆ M，且 j(A) ∩ Vα = A ∩ Vα。

Pick a growth function f. The ticks below δ are its closure points (schematic: faster f, sparser closure points). Click a closure point κ to fire an embedding with critical point κ. Note the order κ < j(f)(κ) < j(κ) (for growth functions, i.e. f(α) > α, as here): since κ is closed under f, elementarity makes j(κ) closed under j(f), and κ < j(κ). 选一个增长函数 f。δ 下方的刻度是它的封闭点（示意：f 越快，封闭点越稀）。点一个封闭点 κ，就以 κ 为临界点“发射”一个嵌入。注意顺序 κ < j(f)(κ) < j(κ)（对增长函数，即 f(α) > α，如这里各例）：κ 对 f 封闭，由初等性 j(κ) 对 j(f) 封闭，而 κ < j(κ)。

Facts ZFC ⊢. (1) Every Woodin cardinal is Mahlo; in fact the measurable cardinals below it form a stationary set. (2) The least Woodin cardinal is not weakly compact, hence not measurable. Being Woodin is a Π¹₁ property over Vδ, so a weakly compact Woodin would reflect it to a smaller Woodin. (3) In consistency strength, Woodin is above strong (below a Woodin δ there are many cardinals that are strong in Vδ) and below superstrong and supercompact. 事实 ZFC ⊢。(1) 每个伍丁基数都是马洛基数；事实上，它下方的可测基数构成一个平稳集。(2) 最小的伍丁基数不是弱紧的，因此也不可测。“是伍丁基数”是 Vδ 上的 Π¹₁ 性质，弱紧的伍丁基数会把这条性质反射到更小的伍丁基数上。(3) 一致性强度上，伍丁高于强基数（伍丁基数 δ 下方有许多在 Vδ 中是强基数的基数），低于超强基数和超紧基数。
⚠ Common pitfall. “Woodin” is not a strengthening of “measurable” for the same cardinal. The witnesses κ are measurable (they are critical points), but δ itself need not be. Also note where the quantifiers sit: for every f there is some κ; one κ need not work for all f. ⚠ 常见误区。“伍丁”并不是同一个基数上对“可测”的加强。见证者 κ 是可测的（它们是临界点），δ 本身却不一定可测。还要注意量词的位置：对每个 f，存在某个 κ；并不要求一个 κ 对所有 f 都管用。
§4

Supercompact and strongly compact: measures on Pκ(λ)超紧与强紧：Pκ(λ) 上的测度

Prerequisites前置: §1 · λM ⊆ M L24 · ultrafilters, normality超滤与正规性
One idea. Look at the image j″λ = {j(α) : α < λ}. Supercompact: M contains this set itself. Strongly compact: M only contains some set D covering it that M thinks is small (|D|M < j(κ)). 核心思想。看像集 j″λ = {j(α) : α < λ}。超紧：M 包含这个集合本身。强紧：M 只需包含一个覆盖它、并且在 M 看来很小的集合 D（|D|M < j(κ)）。
Definitions. Let κ ≤ λ and Pκ(λ) = {x ⊆ λ : |x| < κ}. An ultrafilter U on Pκ(λ) is fine if {x : α ∈ x} ∈ U for every α < λ, and normal if every f with f(x) ∈ x on a U-large set is constant on a U-large set.
• κ is λ-strongly compact ⇔ there is a κ-complete fine ultrafilter on Pκ(λ) ⇔ there is j : V → M with crit(j) = κ and some D ∈ M with j″λ ⊆ D, |D|M < j(κ).
• κ is λ-supercompact ⇔ there is a κ-complete normal fine ultrafilter on Pκ(λ) ⇔ there is j : V → M with crit(j) = κ, j(κ) > λ, λM ⊆ M.
Strongly compact / supercompact = λ-strongly compact / λ-supercompact for all λ ≥ κ. Equivalently, κ (uncountable) is strongly compact iff every κ-complete filter extends to a κ-complete ultrafilter, iff the infinitary logic Lκ,κ satisfies the compactness theorem (that was the original definition). 定义. 设 κ ≤ λ，Pκ(λ) = {x ⊆ λ : |x| < κ}。Pκ(λ) 上的超滤 U 称为精细的，若对每个 α < λ 都有 {x : α ∈ x} ∈ U；称为正规的，若每个在 U-大集上满足 f(x) ∈ x 的函数 f 都在某个 U-大集上取常值。
• κ 是 λ-强紧的 ⇔ Pκ(λ) 上有 κ-完全的精细超滤 ⇔ 存在 j : V → M，crit(j) = κ，且有 D ∈ M 使 j″λ ⊆ D、|D|M < j(κ)。
• κ 是 λ-超紧的 ⇔ Pκ(λ) 上有 κ-完全的正规精细超滤 ⇔ 存在 j : V → M，crit(j) = κ、j(κ) > λ、λM ⊆ M。
强紧 / 超紧 即对所有 λ ≥ κ 都是 λ-强紧 / λ-超紧。等价地，（不可数的）κ 是强紧基数，当且仅当每个 κ-完全滤都能扩张为 κ-完全超滤，当且仅当无穷逻辑 Lκ,κ 满足紧致性定理（这是最初的定义）。

⚠ Common pitfall. Magidor's theorem says the least strongly compact can be the least measurable, so strongly compact does not size-wise sit above measurable. It says nothing about consistency strength: the exact consistency strength of a strongly compact cardinal is unknown (known lower bounds are far above one Woodin cardinal; the known upper bound is a supercompact). ⚠ 常见误区。马吉多尔定理说最小强紧基数可以就是最小可测基数，所以就“大小”而言强紧并不一定在可测之上。但它不涉及一致性强度：强紧基数的确切一致性强度至今未知（已知下界远高于一个伍丁基数，已知上界是超紧基数）。
§5

Extendible and huge: the critical sequence可扩基数与巨大基数：临界序列

Prerequisites前置: §4 · supercompact超紧 L8 · ordinals序数
One idea. Apply j again and again to κ: κ₀ = κ, κ₁ = j(κ), κ₂ = j(κ₁), … This critical sequence measures how far an embedding reaches. Huge: M is closed under κ₁-sequences. n-huge: under κn-sequences. Going all the way to supn κn is impossible (§6). 核心思想。对 κ 反复作用 j：κ₀ = κ, κ₁ = j(κ), κ₂ = j(κ₁), … 这个临界序列刻画了嵌入能“伸”多远。巨大：M 对 κ₁-序列封闭。n-巨大：对 κn-序列封闭。一路走到 supn κn 则不可能（见 §6）。
n =
Definitions. • κ is η-extendible (η > 0) if there are ζ and an elementary j : Vκ+η → Vζ with crit(j) = κ and η < j(κ); κ is extendible if it is η-extendible for all η > 0. (For full extendibility the clause η < j(κ) can be dropped.) Note that j here is a set: a map between two rank initial segments.
• κ is huge if there is j : V → M with crit(j) = κ and j(κ)M ⊆ M; n-huge if jn(κ)M ⊆ M. (1-huge = huge.) Superstrong: Vj(κ) ⊆ M.
Facts ZFC ⊢: extendible ⇒ supercompact. Huge ⇒ superstrong (indeed Vj(κ)+1 ⊆ M). In consistency strength: supercompact < extendible < Vopěnka's principle < huge < 2-huge < 3-huge < …. 定义. • 称 κ 为 η-可扩的（η > 0），若存在 ζ 和初等嵌入 j : Vκ+η → Vζ，使 crit(j) = κ 且 η < j(κ)；若对所有 η > 0 都是 η-可扩的，则称 κ 为可扩基数。（对完整的可扩性，条件 η < j(κ) 可以去掉。）注意这里 j 是一个集合：两个秩初始段之间的映射。
• 若存在 j : V → M，使 crit(j) = κ 且 j(κ)M ⊆ M，则称 κ 为巨大基数；若 jn(κ)M ⊆ M，则称为 n-巨大基数（1-巨大即巨大）。超强基数：Vj(κ) ⊆ M。
事实 ZFC ⊢：可扩 ⇒ 超紧。巨大 ⇒ 超强（事实上 Vj(κ)+1 ⊆ M）。一致性强度：超紧 < 可扩 < 沃彭卡原理 < 巨大 < 2-巨大 < 3-巨大 < ……
⚠ Common pitfall: strength ≠ size. If both exist, the least huge cardinal is smaller than the least supercompact cardinal, and a huge cardinal need not be supercompact. Yet “there is a huge cardinal” has higher consistency strength: it proves the consistency of “there is a supercompact cardinal”. ⚠ 常见误区：强度 ≠ 大小。若两者都存在，最小巨大基数小于最小超紧基数，且巨大基数不一定超紧。然而“存在巨大基数”的一致性强度却更高：它能证明“存在超紧基数”是一致的。
§6

Rank-into-rank and Kunen's wall秩入秩公理与库能之墙

Prerequisites前置: §5 · critical sequence临界序列 L10 · Axiom of Choice选择公理 L15 · L and L(X)L 与 L(X)
One idea. The final step would be M = V, i.e. j : V → V. Kunen proved (using AC) that this is impossible, and more sharply that no j : Vλ+2 → Vλ+2 exists. Just below that line sit the strongest axioms not known to be inconsistent: I3, I2, I1, I0. 核心思想。最后一步将是 M = V，即 j : V → V。库能（利用 AC）证明这不可能，更精确地说，不存在 j : Vλ+2 → Vλ+2。紧挨着这条线的下方，是尚未发现矛盾的最强公理：I3、I2、I1、I0。
Rank-into-rank axioms. In each case j is nontrivial (not the identity), κ = crit(j), and λ = supn κn is the supremum of the critical sequence (for I3 and I1 this is forced by Kunen's theorem).
I3: there is a nontrivial elementary j : Vλ → Vλ.
I2: there is an elementary j : V → M with Vλ ⊆ M, where λ is the supremum of the critical sequence.
I1: there is a nontrivial elementary j : Vλ+1 → Vλ+1.
I0: there is a nontrivial elementary j : L(Vλ+1) → L(Vλ+1) with crit(j) < λ.
Each of I0 ⇒ I1 ⇒ I2 ⇒ I3 holds (in the strong sense that the cardinal witnessing the stronger axiom witnesses the weaker), and I3 is above every n-huge in consistency strength. 秩入秩公理. 以下 j 都非平凡（不是恒等映射），κ = crit(j)，λ = supn κn 是临界序列的上确界（对 I3 和 I1，这一点由库能定理强制）。
I3：存在非平凡初等嵌入 j : Vλ → Vλ。
I2：存在初等嵌入 j : V → M，使 Vλ ⊆ M，其中 λ 是临界序列的上确界。
I1：存在非平凡初等嵌入 j : Vλ+1 → Vλ+1。
I0：存在非平凡初等嵌入 j : L(Vλ+1) → L(Vλ+1)，且 crit(j) < λ。
I0 ⇒ I1 ⇒ I2 ⇒ I3 均成立（而且是强意义下：见证较强公理的基数也见证较弱的），I3 的一致性强度高于任意 n-巨大。
Kunen's inconsistency theorem (1971) ZFC ⊢ ¬. For every ordinal λ there is no nontrivial elementary j : Vλ+2 → Vλ+2. Consequently (in a class theory such as NBG, or with j added to the language and Replacement allowed for formulas mentioning j) there is no nontrivial elementary j : V → V, and no ω-huge cardinal. 库能不一致性定理（1971） ZFC ⊢ ¬。对任意序数 λ，不存在非平凡初等嵌入 j : Vλ+2 → Vλ+2。因此（在 NBG 这样的类理论中，或者把 j 加入语言、并允许含 j 的公式使用替换公理时）不存在非平凡初等嵌入 j : V → V，也不存在 ω-巨大基数。

Proof sketch (Kunen, via ω-Jónsson functions)证明梗概（库能，借助 ω-琼森函数）

Step 1 · Set-up. Suppose j : Vλ+2 → Vλ+2 is elementary and nontrivial, with critical point κ. Let κ₀ = κ, κn+1 = j(κn), and η = supn κn ≤ λ. Because ⟨κn⟩ is a countable sequence and crit(j) > ω, j(⟨κn⟩) = ⟨κn+1⟩, so j(η) = η. 第 1 步 · 准备. 设 j : Vλ+2 → Vλ+2 是非平凡初等嵌入，临界点为 κ。令 κ₀ = κ，κn+1 = j(κn)，η = supn κn ≤ λ。由于 ⟨κn⟩ 是可数序列且 crit(j) > ω，有 j(⟨κn⟩) = ⟨κn+1⟩，所以 j(η) = η。
Step 2 · A combinatorial monster (this is where AC enters). By a theorem of Erdős and Hajnal, which uses the Axiom of Choice, there is an ω-Jónsson function for η: a map f : [η]ω → η such that for every A ⊆ η with |A| = η, f″[A]ω = η. In words, f maps the countable subsets of any large set onto everything. Suitably coded, f is an element of Vλ+2, and “f is ω-Jónsson for η” is expressible there, because it quantifies only over subsets of η. 第 2 步 · 一个组合“怪物”（AC 就在这里用上）. 由埃尔德什–哈伊瑙尔定理（其证明用到选择公理），存在关于 η 的 ω-琼森函数：映射 f : [η]ω → η，使得对每个满足 |A| = η 的 A ⊆ η，都有 f″[A]ω = η。通俗地说，任何一个大集合的可数子集，经 f 映射后能覆盖全部。经过适当编码，f 是 Vλ+2 的元素，且“f 是 η 的 ω-琼森函数”能在其中表达，因为这句话只对 η 的子集作量化。
Step 3 · Elementarity. Then j(f) is ω-Jónsson for j(η) = η. Apply this to A = j″η, which has size η. Hence κ ∈ j(f)″[j″η]ω: there is a countable s ⊆ j″η with j(f)(s) = κ. 第 3 步 · 初等性. 于是 j(f) 是 j(η) = η 的 ω-琼森函数。把它用在 A = j″η 上（其势为 η），得到 κ ∈ j(f)″[j″η]ω：存在可数集 s ⊆ j″η 使 j(f)(s) = κ。
Step 4 · Contradiction. Write s = j″t with t a countable subset of η. Since t is countable and crit(j) > ω, j(t) = j″t = s. So κ = j(f)(j(t)) = j(f(t)), i.e. κ is in the range of j. But κ is not: ordinals α < κ have j(α) = α < κ, and ordinals α ≥ κ have j(α) ≥ j(κ) > κ. Contradiction. ∎
For j : V → V: let λ = sup κn; then j(λ) = λ, so j maps Vλ+2 into itself, and its restriction contradicts what we just proved. (Woodin found a second proof using Solovay's splitting of stationary sets, which also needs AC.) 第 4 步 · 矛盾. 写 s = j″t，其中 t 是 η 的可数子集。由于 t 可数且 crit(j) > ω，j(t) = j″t = s。于是 κ = j(f)(j(t)) = j(f(t))，即 κ 在 j 的值域里。但这不可能：α < κ 时 j(α) = α < κ，α ≥ κ 时 j(α) ≥ j(κ) > κ。矛盾。∎
对 j : V → V：令 λ = sup κn，则 j(λ) = λ，于是 j 把 Vλ+2 映入自身，其限制与刚证明的结论矛盾。（伍丁给出了另一个证明，用的是索洛维的平稳集分割定理，同样需要 AC。）
Beyond the wall: choiceless cardinals. Without AC, Kunen's proof breaks down at Step 2. A Reinhardt cardinal is the critical point of a nontrivial elementary j : V → V (formalized with j as an extra class, e.g. in ZF(j) or NBG-style class theory without choice). A Berkeley cardinal δ: for every transitive set M with δ ∈ M and every α < δ there is a nontrivial elementary j : M → M with α < crit(j) < δ. Berkeley cardinals are stronger than Reinhardt cardinals. Both are inconsistent with ZFC.
open未解决 Whether ZF + “there is a Reinhardt cardinal” (or a Berkeley cardinal) is consistent is open. A known partial result: Suzuki showed in ZF that no definable j : V → V exists. In the other direction, Schlutzenberg (arXiv 2020; J. Math. Logic 2025) showed: if ZF + I0 at λ holds (an elementary j : L(Vλ+1) → L(Vλ+1) with crit(j) < λ), then there is an inner model of ZF + “there is a nontrivial elementary j : Vλ+2 → Vλ+2”. So Con(ZFC + I0) ⇒ Con(ZF + such a j), and the λ+2 bound in Kunen's theorem genuinely needs AC. 墙的另一边：无选择的基数。没有 AC，库能的证明在第 2 步就断了。莱因哈特基数是某个非平凡初等嵌入 j : V → V 的临界点（形式化时把 j 当作额外的类，例如在 ZF(j) 或不含选择的 NBG 式类理论中）。伯克利基数 δ：对每个满足 δ ∈ M 的传递集 M 和每个 α < δ，存在非平凡初等嵌入 j : M → M 使 α < crit(j) < δ。伯克利基数比莱因哈特基数更强。两者都与 ZFC 矛盾。
open未解决 ZF +“存在莱因哈特基数”（或伯克利基数）是否一致，至今未知。已知的部分结果：铃木在 ZF 中证明了不存在可定义的 j : V → V。另一方向上，施卢岑贝格（arXiv 2020；《数理逻辑杂志》2025）证明：若 ZF + 在 λ 处的 I0 成立（存在初等嵌入 j : L(Vλ+1) → L(Vλ+1)，且 crit(j) < λ），则存在满足 ZF +“存在非平凡初等嵌入 j : Vλ+2 → Vλ+2”的内模型。因此 Con(ZFC + I0) ⇒ Con(ZF + 这样的 j)，可见库能定理中 λ+2 这条界线确实依赖 AC。
⚠ Common pitfall. Kunen's theorem does not refute I3–I0: those live at Vλ, Vλ+1 and L(Vλ+1), just under the line Vλ+2. (L(Vλ+1) contains Vλ+1 but generally not all of Vλ+2, and it does not satisfy AC.) And “Reinhardt is inconsistent” is only proved with AC. ⚠ 常见误区。库能定理并没有否定 I3–I0：它们落在 Vλ、Vλ+1 和 L(Vλ+1) 上，正好在 Vλ+2 这条线下方。（L(Vλ+1) 包含 Vλ+1，但一般不包含整个 Vλ+2，而且不满足 AC。）另外，“莱因哈特基数不一致”只是在有 AC 的前提下被证明了。

Finite games and backward induction (Zermelo)有限博弈与逆向归纳（策梅洛）

Prerequisites前置: L3 · ∀∃ alternation∀∃ 交替 L17 · quantifier hierarchy量词层级
One idea. “Player I has a winning strategy” is a long alternating formula ∃a₀ ∀b₀ ∃a₁ ∀b₁ (play ∈ A). For a finite game you can evaluate it from the leaves upward. Every node gets the name of the player who wins from there. 核心思想。“玩家 I 有获胜策略”就是一个很长的交替量词公式 ∃a₀ ∀b₀ ∃a₁ ∀b₁ (对局 ∈ A)。有限博弈可以从叶子往上逐层计算，每个结点都标上从该处出发必胜的玩家。

Player I moves at the root and at depth 2; player II at depths 1 and 3. Click a leaf to change who wins that play. Then press Solve: a node where I is to move is I's if some child is I's; a node where II is to move is I's only if every child is I's. Purple edges show the winner's strategy. 玩家 I 在根和第 2 层走棋，玩家 II 在第 1、3 层走棋。点击叶子可改变该对局的胜者。然后点“求解”：轮到 I 的结点，只要某个子结点归 I，它就归 I；轮到 II 的结点，只有所有子结点都归 I，它才归 I。紫色边是胜者的策略。

Zermelo's theorem ZFC ⊢ (in fact provable in very weak theories). In every finite two-player game of perfect information without draws, exactly one player has a winning strategy. Proof: induction on the height of the game tree, exactly as the animation does it. 策梅洛定理 ZFC ⊢（其实在很弱的理论里就能证明）。在每个没有平局、完全信息的有限二人博弈中，恰有一方有获胜策略。证明：对博弈树的高度归纳，和动画做的完全一样。
⚠ Common pitfall. “Determined” means a winning strategy exists, not that anyone can find it quickly (chess is determined in this sense, with draws as a third outcome). And “exactly one” is easy: if both had winning strategies, play them against each other; the resulting play would be won by both. ⚠ 常见误区。“确定”指获胜策略存在，不等于谁能很快把它找出来（国际象棋在这个意义下也是确定的，只是平局算第三种结局）。“恰有一方”很容易：若双方都有获胜策略，让两个策略对弈，得到的对局就会同时归双方所有，矛盾。
§9

Infinite games: Gale–Stewart, Borel determinacy, and AD versus AC无穷博弈：盖尔–斯图尔特、博雷尔决定性，以及 AD 与 AC 的冲突

Prerequisites前置: §8 L19 · Baire space, Σ⁰₁ / Borel贝尔空间、Σ⁰₁ 与博雷尔集 L1 · diagonal method对角线方法
One idea. Now the play is infinite: I and II alternately pick natural numbers, producing x = (a₀, b₀, a₁, b₁, …) ∈ ωω. Player I wins iff x ∈ A. If A is open, I wins at a finite stage, so the finite-game reasoning survives with ordinal ranks. For arbitrary A, AC builds a game nobody wins. 核心思想。现在对局是无穷的：I 和 II 轮流选自然数，产生 x = (a₀, b₀, a₁, b₁, …) ∈ ωω，当且仅当 x ∈ A 时 I 获胜。若 A 是开集，I 的胜利在某个有限时刻就已注定，于是有限博弈的推理加上序数秩照样适用。对任意的 A，AC 却能造出一个谁都赢不了的博弈。
Definitions. For A ⊆ ωω, the Gale–Stewart game GA: I plays a₀, II plays b₀, I plays a₁, … ; I wins iff (a₀,b₀,a₁,b₁,…) ∈ A. A strategy for I is a function from finite sequences of even length to ω (for II: odd length). It is winning if every play following it is won. GA (or A) is determined if one player has a winning strategy. AD (axiom of determinacy, Mycielski–Steinhaus 1962): every A ⊆ ωω is determined. 定义. 对 A ⊆ ωω，盖尔–斯图尔特博弈 GA：I 出 a₀，II 出 b₀，I 出 a₁，……；当且仅当 (a₀,b₀,a₁,b₁,…) ∈ A 时 I 获胜。I 的策略是从偶数长度有限序列到 ω 的函数（II 的则是奇数长度）。若遵循某策略的每一局都获胜，则称它为获胜策略。若某一方有获胜策略，则称 GA（或 A）是确定的。AD（决定性公理，梅齐尔斯基–施泰因豪斯，1962）：每个 A ⊆ ωω 都是确定的。

(a) Open games: an interactive truncation(a) 开博弈：一个可交互的截断模型

Moves are 0 or 1 and the tree is cut after 5 moves (I moves at even depths, II at odd depths). Click nodes to make them targets: I wins as soon as the play passes through a target, so A is the union of the cylinders [s] of the targets s, an open set. Press “Rank” to compute the positions from which I can force a hit, labelled by the number of moves still needed (in the real ω-branching game these ranks are countable ordinals). Every other position is a “safe” position for II. 着法只有 0 和 1，树在第 5 步之后截断（I 在偶数层走棋，II 在奇数层走棋）。点击结点把它设为目标：对局一旦经过目标，I 立即获胜，所以 A 是各目标 s 的柱集 [s] 之并，是开集。点“计算秩”，求出 I 能强迫命中目标的位置，并标上还需要的步数（在真正 ω 分叉的博弈中，这些秩是可数序数）。其余位置都是 II 的“安全”位置。

Gale–Stewart theorem (1953) ZFC ⊢. Every open (Σ⁰₁) set, and hence every closed (Π⁰₁) set, is determined. 盖尔–斯图尔特定理（1953） ZFC ⊢。每个开集（Σ⁰₁）都是确定的，因而每个闭集（Π⁰₁）也是。
Step 1. Call a position p (finite sequence) good for I if I has a winning strategy in the game continuing from p. Suppose the start is not good for I. We build a winning strategy for II. 第 1 步. 若在从位置 p（有限序列）继续的博弈中 I 有获胜策略，就称 p 对 I 有利。假设起点不是对 I 有利的，我们来为 II 造一个获胜策略。
Step 2. If it is I's turn at a position not good for I, then every move of I leads to a position not good for I (otherwise I would move there and win). If it is II's turn at a position not good for I, then some move of II leads to a position not good for I (otherwise every move of II leads to a good-for-I position, and I could combine those winning strategies, choosing one for each of II's countably many moves, so the position would be good for I; a choice-free version uses the ordinal ranks from the toy above). II's strategy: play the least such move. Choosing the least one needs no choice principle. 第 2 步. 若轮到 I 时位置不对 I 有利，那么 I 的每一步都通向不对 I 有利的位置（否则 I 走过去就赢了）。若轮到 II 时位置不对 I 有利，那么 II 某一步通向不对 I 有利的位置（否则 II 每一步都通向对 I 有利的位置，I 为 II 的可数多种走法各选一个获胜策略拼起来，该位置就对 I 有利了；不用选择的版本可以借助上面玩具模型里的序数秩）。II 的策略是：走最小的这样一步。取“最小”不需要任何选择原理。
Step 3. Following this strategy, every finite stage of the play is a position not good for I. If the final play x were in A, then since A is open, some finite initial segment p of x has [p] ⊆ A. From p, I wins whatever happens, so p is good for I. Contradiction. So x ∉ A and II wins. ∎ (The ranks in the toy above make Step 2 explicit.) 第 3 步. 按此策略，对局的每个有限阶段都是不对 I 有利的位置。如果最终对局 x 属于 A，由于 A 是开集，x 有某个有限前段 p 满足 [p] ⊆ A。从 p 出发 I 怎么走都赢，所以 p 对 I 有利，矛盾。因此 x ∉ A，II 获胜。∎（上面玩具模型里的秩把第 2 步具体化了。）
Borel determinacy (Martin 1975) ZFC ⊢. Every Borel set A ⊆ ωω is determined. The proof “unravels” a Borel game into an open game on a larger tree of moves, and each level of the Borel hierarchy costs further iterations of the power-set operation. Friedman (1971) showed that this cost is unavoidable in an essential way: Borel determinacy cannot be proved in Zermelo set theory (without Replacement), nor in second-order arithmetic. It is a natural statement about sets of reals that genuinely needs uncountably many iterations of the power set. 博雷尔决定性（马丁，1975） ZFC ⊢。每个博雷尔集 A ⊆ ωω 都是确定的。证明把博雷尔博弈“展开”成某棵更大着法树上的开博弈，博雷尔层级每升高一级，都要多迭代几次幂集运算。弗里德曼（1971）证明这一代价本质上不可避免：博雷尔决定性在策梅洛集合论（无替换公理）中不可证，在二阶算术中也不可证。这是一个关于实数集的自然命题，却真正需要不可数多次迭代幂集。

(b) Why AD contradicts AC: a diagonal argument(b) 为什么 AD 与 AC 矛盾：一个对角线论证

AC well-orders all strategies in a list of length 2ℵ₀. At stage α we spoil both the α-th strategy of I and the α-th strategy of II, by putting one play into A and promising another to stay out. This is Cantor's diagonal method run over all strategies. Press “Next stage”. AC 把全体策略良序排成长度为 2ℵ₀ 的一列。第 α 步，同时破坏 I 的第 α 个策略和 II 的第 α 个策略：把一个对局放进 A，再预定另一个对局永远留在 A 外。这就是对全体策略施行的康托尔对角线法。点“下一步”。

Step 1 · Count. A strategy is a function from the countable set ω<ω to ω, so there are exactly ℵ₀ℵ₀ = 2ℵ₀ strategies for each player.第 1 步 · 计数. 策略是从可数集 ω<ω 到 ω 的函数，所以每位玩家恰有 ℵ₀ℵ₀ = 2ℵ₀ 个策略。
Step 2 · Well-order (AC). List I's strategies as ⟨σα : α < 2ℵ₀⟩ and II's as ⟨τα : α < 2ℵ₀⟩, where 2ℵ₀ is taken as an initial ordinal, so every α < 2ℵ₀ has |α| < 2ℵ₀.第 2 步 · 良序（AC）. 把 I 的策略排成 ⟨σα : α < 2ℵ₀⟩，II 的排成 ⟨τα : α < 2ℵ₀⟩，这里 2ℵ₀ 取为初始序数，于是每个 α < 2ℵ₀ 都有 |α| < 2ℵ₀。
Step 3 · Stage α. So far fewer than 2ℵ₀ plays have been chosen. The plays consistent with τα number 2ℵ₀ (I's moves vary freely and are visible in the play), so pick one, xα, not yet promised to stay out, and put it into A. Likewise pick a play yα consistent with σα, different from all xβ (β ≤ α), and promise it stays out of A.第 3 步 · 第 α 步. 目前已选出的对局少于 2ℵ₀ 个。与 τα 相容的对局有 2ℵ₀ 个（I 的着法可以任意变化，而且都出现在对局里），所以能挑一个尚未被预定留在外面的 xα，把它放进 A。同样，挑一个与 σα 相容、且不同于所有 xβ（β ≤ α）的对局 yα，预定它留在 A 外。
Step 4 · Diagonal conclusion. Let A = {xα : α < 2ℵ₀}. No yα lies in A. Each τα loses on xα ∈ A, and each σα loses on yα ∉ A. So no strategy wins GA: A is not determined, and ZFC ⊢ ¬AD. ∎第 4 步 · 对角线结论. 令 A = {xα : α < 2ℵ₀}。没有任何 yα 属于 A。每个 τα 在 xα ∈ A 上输，每个 σα 在 yα ∉ A 上输。所以没有策略能赢 GA：A 不是确定的，于是 ZFC ⊢ ¬AD。∎
AD ⇒ regularity (in ZF). Under AD every set of reals is Lebesgue measurable (Mycielski–Świerczkowski), has the property of Baire (via Banach–Mazur games), and has the perfect set property (Davis, via ∗-games). Each proof turns the property into a game whose winner decides it. The same proofs are local: if every set in a reasonable pointclass Γ is determined, then the sets in Γ (or one level down) are regular. This is how projective determinacy gives projective regularity (§10, L19). AD ⇒ 正则性（在 ZF 中）。在 AD 下，每个实数集都勒贝格可测（梅齐尔斯基–希维尔奇科夫斯基），具有贝尔性质（借助巴拿赫–马祖尔博弈），并具有完美集性质（戴维斯，借助 ∗-博弈）。每个证明都把一条性质改写成博弈，由博弈的胜者来判定它。这些证明还是局部的：若某个合理点类 Γ 中的集合都是确定的，那么 Γ 中（或低一层）的集合就是正则的。投影决定性正是这样推出投影集的正则性（§10，第 19 课）。
⚠ Common pitfall. AD is false in ZFC, but that does not make it useless. The natural statement is ADL(ℝ): “the inner model L(ℝ) satisfies AD”. It is consistent with V satisfying ZFC (relative to large cardinals, §10), since L(ℝ) usually fails AC even when V satisfies it. Also, “open” means open for player I's payoff; closed games are determined by the symmetric argument. ⚠ 常见误区。AD 在 ZFC 中为假，但这不等于它没用。自然的说法是 ADL(ℝ)：“内模型 L(ℝ) 满足 AD”。它可以与“V 满足 ZFC”同时成立（相对于大基数而言，见 §10），因为即使 V 满足 AC，L(ℝ) 通常也不满足。另外，“开”是指 I 的获胜集是开集；闭博弈的确定性由对称的论证得到。
§10

Large cardinals ⇔ determinacy大基数 ⇔ 决定性

Prerequisites前置: L19 · projective hierarchy, regularity投影层级与正则性 §3 · Woodin伍丁 §9
One idea. Each extra Woodin cardinal buys exactly one more level of projective determinacy. The match is so tight that it runs in both directions: determinacy also produces inner models with Woodin cardinals. 核心思想。每多一个伍丁基数，投影决定性就恰好多上一层。两者匹配得如此紧密，以至于反过来也成立：决定性也能造出含伍丁基数的内模型。

Martin; Harrington (1978). Π¹₁-determinacy ⇔ x♯ exists for every real x.马丁；哈灵顿（1978）. Π¹₁-决定性 ⇔ 对每个实数 x，x♯ 存在。ZFC ⊢
Martin–Steel (1989). If there are n Woodin cardinals with a measurable cardinal above them all, then every Π¹n+1 set is determined.马丁–斯蒂尔（1989）. 若存在 n 个伍丁基数，且它们上方还有一个可测基数，则每个 Π¹n+1 集都是确定的。ZFC ⊢
Corollary. Infinitely many Woodin cardinals ⇒ PD (projective determinacy): above the first n Woodins there is another Woodin, and below it a measurable (Woodins are limits of measurables).推论. 无穷多个伍丁基数 ⇒ PD（投影决定性）：前 n 个伍丁基数上方还有一个伍丁基数，而它下方就有可测基数（伍丁基数是可测基数的极限）。ZFC ⊢
Woodin (building on Martin–Steel). If there are infinitely many Woodin cardinals with a measurable above them all, then ADL(ℝ) holds.伍丁（基于马丁–斯蒂尔）. 若存在无穷多个伍丁基数，且它们上方还有一个可测基数，则 ADL(ℝ) 成立。ZFC ⊢
Woodin (converse direction). Π¹n+1-determinacy ⇔ for every real x, the canonical mouse Mn♯(x) with n Woodin cardinals exists and is ω₁-iterable (proof published by Müller–Schindler–Woodin).伍丁（逆方向）. Π¹n+1-决定性 ⇔ 对每个实数 x，含 n 个伍丁基数的典范鼠 Mn♯(x) 存在且 ω₁-可迭代（证明由穆勒–申德勒–伍丁发表）。ZFC ⊢
Woodin. Con(ZF + AD) ⇔ Con(ZFC + there are infinitely many Woodin cardinals). Also, Con(ZFC + PD) ⇔ Con(ZFC + “for each n there are n Woodin cardinals”).伍丁. Con(ZF + AD) ⇔ Con(ZFC + 存在无穷多个伍丁基数)。另有 Con(ZFC + PD) ⇔ Con(ZFC +“对每个 n，存在 n 个伍丁基数”)。≡ Con等一致
In L. If V = L there is a Δ¹₂ well-ordering of the reals, hence a non-measurable Δ¹₂ set, so Π¹₁-determinacy fails. Thus PD (even Π¹₁-Det) is not provable in ZFC (if ZFC is consistent).在 L 中. 若 V = L，则实数有 Δ¹₂ 良序，从而有不可测的 Δ¹₂ 集，于是 Π¹₁-决定性不成立。因此（若 ZFC 一致）ZFC 证不出 PD，连 Π¹₁-Det 都证不出。ZFC ⊢ (the implication)（该蕴涵）
⚠ Common pitfall. Martin–Steel needs something above the n Woodins (a measurable suffices; the optimal hypothesis is that Mn♯(x) exists for every real x). n Woodin cardinals alone do not prove Π¹n+1-determinacy: for n = 0, ZFC does not prove Π¹₁-determinacy. Likewise “ω Woodins ⇒ ADL(ℝ)” uses the measurable above. At the level of consistency, however, no measurable is needed: PD is equiconsistent with “n Woodins for each n”, and AD with ω Woodins. ⚠ 常见误区。马丁–斯蒂尔定理需要 n 个伍丁基数上方还有东西（一个可测基数就够；最优假设是对每个实数 x，Mn♯(x) 存在）。只有 n 个伍丁基数证不出 Π¹n+1-决定性：n = 0 时，ZFC 证不出 Π¹₁-决定性。同样，“ω 个伍丁 ⇒ ADL(ℝ)”用到了上方的可测基数。但在一致性层面不需要可测基数：PD 与“对每个 n 有 n 个伍丁基数”等一致，AD 与 ω 个伍丁基数等一致。

Outlook: what is still open展望：哪些问题仍然悬而未决

Prerequisites前置: L22 · HOD §7 L27 · deep treatment深入讨论 →
One idea. Everything below is open unless marked otherwise. Click a card for a one-paragraph status report; Lesson 27 treats these in depth. 核心思想。除非另有标注，下面每一项都尚未解决。点开卡片看一段状态说明；第 27 课会深入讨论。
Philosophical status. Large-cardinal axioms are not provable in ZFC (an inaccessible κ gives a model Vκ of ZFC, so by Gödel II, ZFC cannot prove one exists, assuming ZFC is consistent). Why adopt them? Realists see them as describing the full, maximal universe of sets, and point to extrinsic evidence: they settle projective questions (§10), and they are linearly ordered and mutually reinforcing. Formalists regard set theory as whatever follows from the chosen axioms, with no reason to privilege these extensions. Some realists even suspect certain large cardinals are false. The SEP entry on large cardinals and determinacy records the debate between “non-pluralists” (e.g. Martin, Steel, Woodin) and pluralists (e.g. Feferman, Shelah). 哲学地位。大基数公理在 ZFC 中不可证（不可达基数 κ 给出 ZFC 的模型 Vκ，由哥德尔第二不完全性定理，若 ZFC 一致，则 ZFC 证不出它存在）。那为何要接受它们？实在论者认为它们刻画的是完整、极大的集合宇宙，并举出外在证据：它们解决了投影集问题（§10），而且彼此线性有序、相互印证。形式主义者则认为集合论就是所选公理的推论，没有理由偏爱这些扩张。有些实在论者甚至怀疑某些大基数公理是假的。斯坦福哲学百科“大基数与决定性”条目记录了“非多元论者”（如马丁、斯蒂尔、伍丁）与多元论者（如费弗曼、谢拉赫）之间的争论。
⚠ Common pitfall. “Conjecture” ≠ “theorem”. The HOD Dichotomy is a theorem; the HOD Conjecture is open. “Inner models for supercompacts” is a program, not a result. ⚠ 常见误区。“猜想”≠“定理”。HOD 二分定理是已证定理；HOD 猜想尚未解决。“超紧基数的内模型”是一项研究纲领，不是已有结果。
