A scrubbable timeline, 1870 to today可拖动的时间轴：1870 年至今

Related相关: L6 · Russell & ZF罗素与 ZF L12 · incompleteness不完全性 L16 · forcing力迫法
One idea. Almost every major result in logic was an answer to a concrete question someone was stuck on. Read the history as a chain of problems, not a list of names. 核心思想。逻辑学里几乎每个重大结果，都是在回答某人卡住的一个具体问题。把这段历史读成一串问题，而不是一串人名。

Summary. Roughly: 1870–1910 the infinite becomes mathematics and paradoxes appear; 1910–1940 axioms are fixed and the limits of formal methods are proved (completeness, incompleteness, undecidability, undefinability of truth); 1940–1975 independence and the four branches mature; 1975–today large cardinals and determinacy, deep applications to algebra, geometry, number theory and analysis, and machine-checked mathematics. 小结。大致说来：1870–1910 年，无穷成为数学对象，悖论随之出现；1910–1940 年，公理被确立下来，形式方法的极限被证明（完备性、不完全性、不可判定性、真的不可定义性）；1940–1975 年，独立性结果出现，四大分支走向成熟；1975 年至今，大基数与决定性理论成形，逻辑在代数、几何、数论、分析中获得深刻应用，计算机验证的数学兴起。
⚠ Common pitfall. “Hilbert's program was refuted in 1931, so it was a failure.” Gödel showed the original goal (a finitary consistency proof of all of mathematics) is impossible. But the program created proof theory, and modified versions (Gentzen's ordinal analysis, reverse mathematics) are still active research. ⚠ 常见误区。“希尔伯特纲领 1931 年被驳倒了，所以是失败的。”哥德尔证明的是原始目标（用有穷方法证明全部数学的一致性）不可能实现。但这个纲领催生了证明论，它的修正版本（根岑的序数分析、反推数学）至今仍是活跃的研究方向。
§2

Why foundations? The “crisis”, told carefully, and three motivations为什么研究基础？审慎地讲“危机”，以及三种动机

Related相关: L6 · Russell's paradox罗素悖论 L8 · ordinals序数 L10 · choice选择公理
One idea. The paradoxes broke specific systems (naive comprehension, Frege's Basic Law V), not mathematics as a whole. The lasting motivations for foundations are three: securing mathematics, understanding the power and limits of formal methods, and studying the infinite for its own sake. 核心思想。悖论击垮的是具体的系统（朴素概括原则、弗雷格的第五基本定律），而不是整个数学。研究基础的持久动机有三：为数学奠定可靠基础，弄清形式方法的能力与极限，以及把无穷本身当作数学来研究。

Part A · What actually cracked?A 部分 · 到底是什么裂开了？

The building has three floors. Click an episode and watch which floor is hit. 这座楼有三层。点击一段历史，看哪一层受到冲击。

⚠ Historical care. The word “crisis” (Grundlagenkrise) was popularized by Hermann Weyl's 1921 paper “On the new foundational crisis of mathematics”, which sided with Brouwer; Hilbert replied in 1922. Most working mathematicians did not stop proving theorems. Calculus, number theory and algebra were never shown to be inconsistent. What the paradoxes showed is that unrestricted set formation is contradictory, so the rules for forming sets must be stated precisely. ⚠ 史实要审慎。“危机”（Grundlagenkrise）一词因赫尔曼·外尔 1921 年的论文《论数学的新基础危机》而流行，该文站在布劳威尔一边；希尔伯特于 1922 年作出回应。大多数数学家并没有停下来不证定理，微积分、数论、代数也从未被证明是矛盾的。悖论真正说明的是：不加限制地构造集合会导致矛盾，所以构造集合的规则必须精确地写出来。

Part B · Three motivations: place each resultB 部分 · 三种动机：给每个结果找位置

Each circle is a motivation. Click a result chip to see where it lives and why. Many results serve more than one motivation, which is why the circles overlap. 每个圆代表一种动机。点击一个结果，看它落在哪里、为什么。很多结果同时服务于几种动机，所以圆会重叠。

Summary. (1) Security: fix precise axioms (ZFC) and ask whether they are consistent. Gödel's second theorem (1931) says a consistent, effectively axiomatized theory containing enough arithmetic cannot prove its own consistency, so security becomes a matter of relative consistency and evidence. (2) Power and limits: what can be proved, computed, defined? Answers: completeness, incompleteness, undecidability, undefinability of truth. (3) The infinite as mathematics: ordinals, cardinals, large cardinals, determinacy, forcing. 小结。(1) 可靠性：确立精确的公理（ZFC），追问它是否一致。哥德尔第二不完全性定理（1931）表明：一致的、能行公理化的、包含足够算术的理论不能证明自身的一致性，于是“可靠”只能以相对一致性和各种证据的形式出现。(2) 能力与极限：什么能证明、能计算、能定义？答案是完备性、不完全性、不可判定性、真的不可定义性。(3) 作为数学的无穷：序数、基数、大基数、决定性、力迫法。
§3

The four pillars and the bridges between them四大支柱以及连接它们的桥梁

Related相关: L4 · completeness完备性 L11 · computability可计算性 L14 · models of ZFCZFC 的模型
One idea. Set theory, model theory, computability theory and proof theory (the traditional four, as in the 1977 Handbook of Mathematical Logic) are not separate subjects. They are four viewpoints on one object, the relation between formal language and mathematical structure, and their deepest theorems are bridges. 核心思想。集合论、模型论、可计算性理论、证明论（传统的四分法，见 1977 年的《数理逻辑手册》）并不是彼此孤立的学科。它们是从四个角度观察同一个对象，即形式语言与数学结构之间的关系；它们最深刻的定理往往就是桥梁。

Click a pillar for its core question, tools, flagship theorems and lessons. Click an edge (a labeled bridge) to see the theorem that connects two pillars. 点击支柱，查看它的核心问题、主要工具、代表性定理和相关课程。点击连线（带标签的桥），查看把两根支柱连起来的定理。

Summary. Model theory: truth in structures. Proof theory: proofs as objects. Computability: what algorithms can do. Set theory: the universe of sets and the infinite. Bridges: completeness (proofs ↔ models), incompleteness ↔ undecidability (proofs ↔ algorithms), Boolean-valued models / ultrapowers / infinitary compactness (models ↔ sets), ordinal analysis (proofs ↔ ordinals), effective descriptive set theory (algorithms ↔ definable sets of reals), large cardinals ↔ determinacy. 小结。模型论研究结构中的真；证明论研究作为对象的证明；可计算性理论研究算法能做什么；集合论研究集合宇宙与无穷。桥梁：完备性定理（证明 ↔ 模型），不完全性 ↔ 不可判定性（证明 ↔ 算法），布尔值模型、超幂、无穷语言的紧致性（模型 ↔ 集合），序数分析（证明 ↔ 序数），能行描述集合论（算法 ↔ 可定义实数集），大基数 ↔ 决定性。
⚠ Common pitfall. The four-way split is a convenient teaching map, not a law of nature. Many researchers work on bridges (e.g. computable model theory, set-theoretic model theory, proof mining), and some areas (constructive mathematics, type theory, categorical logic) do not fit neatly into any single pillar. ⚠ 常见误区。四分法只是便于教学的地图，不是自然规律。许多研究者恰恰在桥上工作（如可计算模型论、集合论化的模型论、证明挖掘），也有一些领域（构造性数学、类型论、范畴逻辑）很难归入某一根支柱。
§4

Recurring theme 1: the diagonal method反复出现的主题一：对角线方法

Related相关: L1 §8 L11 · halting problem停机问题 L12 · Gödel哥德尔 L13 · Tarski塔斯基 L16 · forcing力迫法
One idea. Take a list indexed by the same things it talks about, look at the diagonal, and flip it. The flipped diagonal cannot be on the list. The same move proves Cantor's theorem, Russell's paradox, the halting problem, Gödel's and Tarski's theorems. 核心思想。拿一张“用它所谈论的对象本身来编号”的表，看对角线，再把它翻转。翻转后的对角线不可能出现在表里。同一个动作证明了康托尔定理、罗素悖论、停机问题、哥德尔定理和塔斯基定理。

Pick an instance. The grid relabels itself; press “Flip the diagonal” to watch the argument run. 选一个实例，网格会换上相应的标签；按“翻转对角线”观看论证过程。

Summary (Lawvere's fixed-point theorem, 1969, abstracts the pattern). If e : A → (A → B) is surjective (every function A → B is some row), then every map f : B → B has a fixed point. Contrapositive: if some f has no fixed point (like 0 ↦ 1, 1 ↦ 0), no list can be complete. Gödel and Tarski use the positive form: the diagonal lemma produces a fixed point (a self-referential sentence). 小结（劳维尔不动点定理，1969，把这一模式抽象出来）。若 e : A → (A → B) 是满射（每个函数 A → B 都是表中某一行），则每个映射 f : B → B 都有不动点。逆否形式：只要某个 f 没有不动点（如 0 ↦ 1, 1 ↦ 0），任何表都不可能完备。哥德尔和塔斯基用的是正面形式：对角线引理造出一个不动点（一个自指语句）。
⚠ Common pitfall. Forcing is not literally a diagonal argument. The analogy is that a generic object is built to meet countably many requirements (dense sets) of a countable model, and in particular it differs from every real in that model. That “defeat each requirement in turn” idea is diagonalization in the broad sense, as is the priority method in computability theory. ⚠ 常见误区。力迫法并不严格是对角线论证。相似之处在于：泛型对象被构造来满足可数模型中可数多个要求（稠密集），特别地，它与该模型中的每个实数都不同。“逐个击破要求”正是广义的对角化思想，可计算性理论中的优先方法也是如此。
§5

Recurring theme 2: syntax vs semantics (⊢ vs ⊨)反复出现的主题二：语法与语义（⊢ 与 ⊨）

Related相关: L2 · soundness, completeness可靠性、完备性 L4 · Gödel completeness哥德尔完备性 L12 · incompleteness不完全性
One idea. There are two notions of consequence: ⊢ (there is a finite formal proof) and ⊨ (true in every model). Whether they coincide depends on the logic, and “complete” means two different things in the completeness and incompleteness theorems. 核心思想。后承有两种：⊢（存在有限的形式证明）与 ⊨（在所有模型中为真）。二者是否重合取决于所用的逻辑；而且“完备”在完备性定理与不完全性定理里是两个不同的意思。

Choose a setting. Green bridges hold, red bridges are provably broken. 选择一种情形。绿色的桥成立，红色的桥已被证明是断的。

Summary. Completeness theorem Gödel 1929/30: for first-order logic, T ⊢ φ ⇔ T ⊨ φ (φ holds in every model of T). First incompleteness theorem Gödel 1931: a consistent, effectively axiomatized T containing enough arithmetic is not complete, i.e. some sentence φ has T ⊬ φ and T ⊬ ¬φ. These are compatible: such a φ is true in some models of T and false in others. 小结。完备性定理 Gödel 1929/30哥德尔 1929/30：在一阶逻辑中，T ⊢ φ ⇔ T ⊨ φ（φ 在 T 的每个模型中成立）。第一不完全性定理 Gödel 1931哥德尔 1931：一致、能行公理化且包含足够算术的理论 T 不是完全的，即存在语句 φ 使 T ⊬ φ 且 T ⊬ ¬φ。两者并不矛盾：这样的 φ 在 T 的某些模型中为真，在另一些模型中为假。

Recurring theme 3: ladders everywhere反复出现的主题三：处处是阶梯

Related相关: L17L18L19L20L21L23L24L25
One idea. Logic measures complexity by hierarchies: count quantifier alternations, iterate the jump, climb the ordinals, add stronger axioms. Part V built seven such ladders; the surprise is that they are linked rung by rung by precise theorems. 核心思想。逻辑用层级来度量复杂性：数量词交替的次数、迭代图灵跳跃、沿序数往上爬、加入更强的公理。第五部分建起了七架这样的阶梯；令人惊讶的是，它们被精确的定理一级一级地连在一起。

Click a bridge button to light up the rungs it connects. Click any rung to jump to its lesson. 点击桥梁按钮，点亮它所连接的梯级。点击任一梯级可跳到对应课程。

Summary. Post's theorem: Σ⁰ₙ₊₁ = c.e. relative to 0⁽ⁿ⁾. Kleene / Suslin: Δ¹₁ = hyperarithmetic, and boldface Δ¹₁ = Borel. Shoenfield: Σ¹₂ statements are absolute (a Lévy-Σ₁-type phenomenon). Gentzen: ε₀ measures PA. Martin, Martin–Steel: measurable ⇒ Σ¹₁-determinacy; n Woodin cardinals with a measurable above ⇒ Π¹ₙ₊₁-determinacy. 小结。波斯特定理：Σ⁰ₙ₊₁ = 相对于 0⁽ⁿ⁾ 可计算枚举。克莱尼／苏斯林：Δ¹₁ = 超算术，粗体 Δ¹₁ = 博雷尔。勋菲尔德：Σ¹₂ 命题是绝对的（与列维 Σ₁ 绝对性同类的现象）。根岑：ε₀ 度量 PA 的强度。马丁、马丁–斯蒂尔：可测基数 ⇒ Σ¹₁ 决定性；n 个伍丁基数加其上一个可测基数 ⇒ Π¹ₙ₊₁ 决定性。
⚠ Common pitfall. The ladders are drawn side by side, but their rungs are not “equal”. A bridge is a specific theorem linking specific rungs; heights in the picture are only schematic. ⚠ 常见误区。这些阶梯并排画着，但梯级之间并不“相等”。每座桥都是连接特定梯级的具体定理；图中的高度只是示意。
§7

Recurring theme 4: independence as a phenomenon反复出现的主题四：作为现象的独立性

Related相关: L12L15 · LL16 · forcing力迫法L25 · PD
One idea. “Independent” is always relative to a theory. Moving to a stronger theory can settle a statement (Goodstein's theorem, Con(PA), PD) or leave it open (CH, by all known large cardinals). Independence is a precise theorem, not a shrug. 核心思想。“独立”永远是相对于某个理论而言的。换成更强的理论，有的命题就被判定了（古德斯坦定理、Con(PA)、PD），有的仍然悬而未决（CH，已知的大基数都判定不了）。独立性是一个精确的定理，而不是一句“说不清”。

Each row is a statement, each column a theory. Reveal cells one at a time (click), or reveal a whole column. Click a revealed cell for the justification. 每行是一个命题，每列是一个理论。逐格点击揭晓，或整列揭晓。再次点击已揭晓的格子可查看依据。

Summary. “S is independent of T” means T ⊬ S and T ⊬ ¬S (usually proved under a consistency hypothesis). For arithmetic sentences of the simplest form (Π⁰₁, like Goldbach), independence from a true theory that contains basic arithmetic (e.g. PA or ZFC) would imply truth in ℕ. For CH there is no such shortcut, and whether it has a definite answer is debated (L27). 小结。“S 独立于 T”是指 T ⊬ S 且 T ⊬ ¬S（通常是在某个一致性假设下证明的）。对于最简单形式（Π⁰₁，如哥德巴赫猜想）的算术语句，若它独立于某个包含基本算术的真理论（如 PA 或 ZFC），则它在 ℕ 中必然为真。对 CH 没有这样的捷径，它是否有确定答案仍有争论（见第 27 课）。
⚠ Common pitfalls. “Independence = no answer” and “CH is meaningless” are both philosophical positions, not theorems. Many set theorists think CH has a definite truth value and look for well-justified new axioms to decide it; others (multiverse views) think different universes with different answers are equally legitimate. ⚠ 常见误区。“独立 = 没有答案”和“CH 没有意义”都是哲学立场，不是定理。许多集合论学家认为 CH 有确定的真值，并寻找有充分理由的新公理来判定它；另一些人（多重宇宙观）则认为给出不同答案的各个宇宙同样合法。
§8

What logic does for mainstream mathematics逻辑为主流数学做了什么

Related相关: L5 · compactness紧致性 L19 · Borel sets博雷尔集 L28
One idea. Logic is not only about logic. Model theory proves theorems in number theory and geometry, set theory decides (or shows undecidable) questions in algebra and analysis, computability shows some natural problems have no algorithm, and proof theory extracts numbers from non-constructive proofs. 核心思想。逻辑不只研究逻辑本身。模型论证明了数论和几何中的定理，集合论判定（或证明不可判定）代数和分析中的问题，可计算性理论表明一些自然的问题没有算法，证明论则能从非构造性证明中提取出具体的数值。

Filter by the pillar that does the work, then click a card. 按“出力”的支柱筛选，然后点开卡片。

Summary. Patterns: (a) transfer (Ax–Kochen: a statement true in one class of fields transfers to another for all but finitely many p); (b) tameness (o-minimality, stability: definable sets behave well, so counting or geometry works); (c) independence (Whitehead's problem); (d) impossibility (Hilbert's 10th problem; non-classifiability of ergodic transformations); (e) calibration and extraction (reverse mathematics, proof mining); (f) certainty (formal verification). 小结。几种模式：(a) 转移（Ax–Kochen：在一类域中成立的命题，除有限多个 p 外可转移到另一类域）；(b) 驯顺性（o-极小性、稳定性：可定义集性质良好，于是计数或几何论证得以进行）；(c) 独立性（怀特海问题）；(d) 不可能性（希尔伯特第十问题；遍历变换不可分类）；(e) 校准与提取（反推数学、证明挖掘）；(f) 确定性（形式化验证）。
⚠ Common pitfall. Ax–Kochen did not prove Artin's conjecture; the conjecture is false in general (Terjanian, 1966). They proved that it holds for each degree d for all but finitely many primes p. ⚠ 常见误区。Ax–Kochen 并没有证明阿廷猜想；该猜想一般来说是错的（Terjanian，1966）。他们证明的是：对每个次数 d，除有限多个素数 p 外猜想成立。
§9

Philosophical positions: where do you stand?哲学立场：你站在哪里？

Related相关: L12L16L27 · CH
One idea. Theorems do not settle philosophy. Platonists, formalists, intuitionists, structuralists, multiverse theorists and naturalists all accept the same theorems of ZFC (intuitionists: their constructive versions), and disagree about what the theorems are about and which new axioms are justified. 核心思想。定理并不能裁决哲学。柏拉图主义者、形式主义者、直觉主义者、结构主义者、多重宇宙论者和自然主义者都接受同样的 ZFC 定理（直觉主义者接受的是其构造性版本），他们分歧在于这些定理谈论的是什么，以及哪些新公理是有道理的。

Answer the five questions. Your answers are scored against six positions. There is no right answer, and the descriptions below are written to be neutral. 回答五个问题，你的答案会和六种立场比对计分。这里没有标准答案，下面的介绍力求中立。

The six positions (click to expand)六种立场（点击展开）

Summary. These are positions, not theorems. They overlap (e.g. one can be a structuralist and a realist). Their practical bite shows up in two places: which methods count as proof (intuitionism rejects the law of excluded middle in general), and how to justify new axioms such as large cardinals or answers to CH. 小结。这些都是position立场，不是定理。它们之间可以重叠（例如既是结构主义者又是实在论者）。它们在实践中的分歧主要体现在两处：哪些方法算作证明（直觉主义一般地拒绝排中律），以及如何为新公理辩护（例如大基数，或对 CH 的回答）。
§10

Alternative foundations其他的数学基础

Related相关: L6 · ZFL10 · ACL1 · Peirce's law皮尔士定律
One idea. ZFC is the standard reference foundation, but not the only one. Other foundations take a different primitive notion (types, functions/morphisms, homotopy types, constructions) because they care about different things: computation, invariance under isomorphism, or machine checking. Most mathematics can be done in all of them, and their relative strengths are well studied. 核心思想。ZFC 是标准的参照基础，但不是唯一的基础。其他基础以不同的概念为原始概念（类型、函数／态射、同伦类型、构造），因为它们关心的东西不同：可计算性、同构不变性或机器检验。大多数数学在这些框架里都能做，它们之间的相对强度也已被深入研究。

Summary. Type theories and HoTT are motivated by computation and formal proof; category-theoretic foundations by “structure is what matters”; Bishop-style constructive mathematics by computational content. None of them “refutes” ZFC: each can be compared with ZFC by interpretations and consistency-strength results. 小结。类型论与 HoTT 的动机来自计算和形式证明；范畴论基础的动机是“结构才是关键”；毕晓普式构造性数学的动机是计算内容。它们都没有“驳倒”ZFC：每一种都可以通过解释和一致性强度的结果与 ZFC 作比较。
⚠ Common pitfall. “Constructive mathematics says excluded middle is false.” Bishop's constructive mathematics simply does not use it; every theorem is also classically valid. Brouwer's intuitionism is different: it adds principles (e.g. continuity principles) that contradict classical mathematics. ⚠ 常见误区。“构造性数学认为排中律是错的。”毕晓普的构造性数学只是不使用排中律，其每个定理在经典意义下也成立。布劳威尔的直觉主义则不同：它加入了与经典数学相矛盾的原则（如连续性原则）。
§11

What research looks like today今天的研究是什么样的

Next后续: L27 · set theory frontiers集合论前沿 L28 · model theory, computability, proof theory frontiers模型论、可计算性、证明论前沿
One idea. Each subfield has a few “big questions” that organize its research. Here each is compressed into one sentence, with a status tag; the next two lessons unpack them. 核心思想。每个分支都有几个统领研究的“大问题”。这里把每个问题压缩成一句话并标明状态；接下来的两课再逐一展开。

Click a question for one more sentence of context.点击问题，查看一句补充说明。

Summary. The open problems are of three kinds: mathematical (a precise statement, true or false, e.g. Martin's conjecture), programmatic (find the right axioms or methods, e.g. inner models for supercompact cardinals), and foundational (what counts as a justified axiom, e.g. CH). Keep the three apart. 小结。未解问题有三类：数学问题（精确的命题，非真即假，如马丁猜想）、纲领性问题（找到合适的公理或方法，如超紧基数的内模型）、基础性问题（什么才算有理由的公理，如 CH）。三者要分开看待。
