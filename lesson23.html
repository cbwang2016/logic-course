Part V · Lesson 23

Large cardinals I: from inaccessible to weakly compact大基数（一）：从不可达到弱紧

ZFC can build ℵ₁, ℵω, ℵℵω, … but some cardinals are so large that no ZFC operation applied to smaller sets ever reaches them. This lesson climbs the first rungs of that ladder in 11 small steps. Each step has one idea, one picture to play with, one exact definition, and one quick self-check. ZFC 能造出 ℵ₁、ℵω、ℵℵω……但有些基数大到这种程度：对更小的集合施行任何 ZFC 运算，都够不着它们。本课分 11 小步爬上这架梯子的前几级。每一步只讲一个想法，配一张能动手的图、一个精确定义和一道自测题。

Step 1

What is a “large cardinal”?什么是“大基数”？

Prerequisites:预备： L9 cardinals, cofinality基数与共尾性 L12 Gödel 2哥德尔第二定理 L14 Vα累积层级

Core idea: a large cardinal is one you cannot reach from below with ZFC's operations.核心想法：大基数是用 ZFC 的运算从下面够不着的基数。

Start at ℵ₀ and press the buttons. They are the three ways ZFC climbs: take the next cardinal, take the power set, or take the supremum of a shorter sequence. Pick which κ you are trying to reach. 从 ℵ₀ 出发按按钮。这三个按钮就是 ZFC 往上爬的三种方式：取下一个基数、取幂集、取一段较短序列的上确界。先选好你要够的目标 κ。

Working criteria (partial definition, following Wikipedia). There is no universally accepted formal definition of “large cardinal property”. A necessary condition for a property P of cardinals to count as one is:
(i) the existence of a cardinal with P is not known to be inconsistent with ZF(C); and
(ii) (ZFC proves that) any such κ is an uncountable initial ordinal with Lκ ⊨ ZFC.
Consequence (iii): if ZFC is consistent, ZFC does not prove that such a κ exists. Reason: by (ii), ZFC + “∃κ P(κ)” proves “ZFC has a set model”, hence Con(ZFC). If ZFC itself proved ∃κ P(κ), then ZFC ⊢ Con(ZFC), and by Gödel's second incompleteness theorem ZFC would be inconsistent. 工作判据（不完整定义，依维基百科）. “大基数性质”并没有公认的形式定义。一个基数性质 P 要算作大基数性质，必要条件是：
(i) “存在具有 P 的基数”目前不知道与 ZF(C) 矛盾；
(ii) （ZFC 可证）任何这样的 κ 都是不可数的初始序数，并且 Lκ ⊨ ZFC。
推论 (iii)：若 ZFC 一致，则 ZFC 证明不了这样的 κ 存在。理由：由 (ii)，ZFC + “∃κ P(κ)” 能证明“ZFC 有集合模型”，从而证明 Con(ZFC)。如果 ZFC 自己就能证明 ∃κ P(κ)，那么 ZFC ⊢ Con(ZFC)，由哥德尔第二不完全性定理，ZFC 不一致。
Why does (ii) say Lκ and not Vκ? Because a weakly inaccessible κ (Step 3) always has Lκ ⊨ ZFC, yet Vκ ⊨ ZFC can fail for it (Step 4). The L-version covers both. 为什么 (ii) 写的是 Lκ 而不是 Vκ？因为弱不可达基数 κ（第 3 步）总有 Lκ ⊨ ZFC，而 Vκ ⊨ ZFC 却可能不成立（第 4 步）。用 L 的版本能把两者都包括进来。

Three stances (philosophy, not mathematics)三种立场（属于哲学，不是数学定理）

Maximalist / realist. V is built by iterating the full power set; large cardinal axioms say we are looking at all the sets, and their negations are restrictive (cut V off at the first inaccessible, or pass to L). They get a preferred status among extensions of ZFC; hard-core realists simply call them true. 极大主义／实在论. V 由反复取完整幂集生成；大基数公理说的是我们考虑了所有集合，而它们的否定是“限制性的”（在第一个不可达处把 V 截断，或者退到 L）。因此大基数公理在 ZFC 的各种扩张中享有优先地位；坚定的实在论者干脆说它们是真的。
Formalist. Set theory studies what follows from ZFC (and other systems). There is no reason to single out large cardinal axioms as preferred. 形式主义. 集合论研究从 ZFC（及其他系统）能推出什么，没有理由特别偏爱大基数公理。
Dissent. Some realists reject “maximize the universe” as a motive and even think large cardinal axioms are false. Others deny that the negations are restrictive: e.g. a transitive set model inside L can believe a measurable cardinal exists, though L itself does not. 异议. 有的实在论者不接受“让宇宙尽量大”这一动机，甚至认为大基数公理是假的。还有人否认其否定是限制性的：例如 L 内部可以有一个传递集合模型相信存在可测基数，尽管 L 本身并不满足这一点。
⚠ Common pitfall常见误区
“ZFC can't prove an inaccessible exists” does not mean “ZFC proves no inaccessible exists”. Both are unprovable (if ZFC is consistent). The first follows from Gödel 2; the second from the fact that cutting V at the least inaccessible gives a model with none (Step 4). “ZFC 证明不了不可达基数存在”不等于“ZFC 证明了不可达基数不存在”。（若 ZFC 一致）两者都证明不了：前者来自哥德尔第二定理，后者来自“在最小不可达处截断 V，得到一个没有不可达基数的模型”（第 4 步）。
Step 2

Two ladders: consistency strength vs size两架梯子：一致性强度与大小

Prerequisites:预备： L12 Con(T)Con(T) Step 1第 1 步

Core idea: “stronger” (proves more consistency) and “bigger” (least witness is larger) are two different orders.核心想法：“更强”（能证明更多一致性）和“更大”（最小实例更大）是两种不同的序。

Definitions. Let A, B be large cardinal axioms.
• A is consistency-wise stronger than B if ZFC + A ⊢ Con(ZFC + B).
• A and B are equiconsistent if Con(ZFC + A) ⇔ Con(ZFC + B) is provable (in a weak base theory such as PA).
Observation (Wikipedia), not a theorem: for every pair of large cardinal axioms studied so far, exactly one of these holds: equiconsistent; A stronger; B stronger (assuming the theories are consistent). It is not even known in every case which one holds. Without a formal definition of “large cardinal”, this cannot be a theorem; Woodin derives it from his still-open Ω-conjecture. 定义. 设 A、B 是大基数公理。
• 若 ZFC + A ⊢ Con(ZFC + B)，称 A 的一致性强度高于 B。
• 若 Con(ZFC + A) ⇔ Con(ZFC + B) 可证（在 PA 这类较弱的基础理论中），称 A 与 B 等相容。
这是观察，不是定理（依维基百科）：迄今研究过的任意两条大基数公理，（在理论一致的前提下）都恰好满足三者之一：等相容；A 更强；B 更强。而且并非每一对都已知是哪一种。由于“大基数”本身没有形式定义，这无法成为定理；伍丁从他尚未解决的 Ω 猜想推出了它。

Click a name on either ladder. Left: ordered by consistency strength (higher = stronger). Right: ordered by the size of the least witness, assuming all of them exist. 点击任一架梯子上的名字。左边按一致性强度排（越高越强）；右边按最小实例的大小排（假设它们都存在）。

⚠ Common pitfall常见误区
“The least huge cardinal is smaller than the least supercompact” does not make “huge” the weaker axiom. In fact huge is much stronger. Size and strength are different orders. “最小的巨大基数比最小的超紧基数小”并不意味着“巨大基数”是更弱的公理。事实上巨大基数的一致性强度高得多。大小与强度是两种不同的序。
Step 3

Weakly vs strongly inaccessible弱不可达与强不可达

Prerequisites:预备： L9 regular / singular正则／奇异 Step 1第 1 步

Core idea: be uncountable, be regular, and be a limit. Each candidate below fails at least one test, except the last.核心想法：不可数、正则、是极限，三关都要过。下面的候选者除最后一个外，每个都至少挂掉一关。

Definitions (κ an infinite cardinal).
• κ is regular if cf(κ) = κ; otherwise singular.
• κ is a (weak) limit cardinal if it is not a successor cardinal: λ⁺ < κ for every cardinal λ < κ. (Conventions differ on ℵ₀; here ℵ₀ counts as a limit cardinal.)
• κ is a strong limit if 2λ < κ for every cardinal λ < κ.
• κ is weakly inaccessible if it is uncountable, regular, and a limit cardinal.
• κ is (strongly) inaccessible if it is uncountable, regular, and a strong limit. 定义（κ 为无穷基数）。
• 若 cf(κ) = κ，称 κ 正则；否则称奇异。
• 若 κ 不是后继基数，即对每个基数 λ < κ 都有 λ⁺ < κ，称 κ 为（弱）极限基数。（ℵ₀ 算不算有不同约定，这里算。）
• 若对每个基数 λ < κ 都有 2λ < κ，称 κ 为强极限基数。
• 不可数、正则、且是极限基数，称为弱不可达。
• 不可数、正则、且是强极限基数，称为（强）不可达。

Click a cardinal on the ladder. The table explains every verdict. A yellow “?” means ZFC cannot decide it. 点击梯子上的基数，表格会解释每一项判定。黄色“?”表示 ZFC 无法判定。

Facts. (1) Strong limit ⇒ limit, since λ⁺ ≤ 2λ. So strongly inaccessible ⇒ weakly inaccessible. (2) Under GCH, 2λ = λ⁺, so the two notions coincide. (3) Theorem. If κ is weakly inaccessible then κ = ℵκ. 事实. (1) 强极限 ⇒ 极限，因为 λ⁺ ≤ 2λ。所以强不可达 ⇒ 弱不可达。(2) 在 GCH 下 2λ = λ⁺，两个概念重合。(3) 定理. 若 κ 弱不可达，则 κ = ℵκ。

Proof of (3)(3) 的证明

Write κ = ℵα. Since κ is a limit cardinal and uncountable, α is a limit ordinal.写 κ = ℵα。κ 不可数且是极限基数，所以 α 是极限序数。
⟨ℵβ : β < α⟩ is cofinal in ℵα, so cf(κ) = cf(α) ≤ α.⟨ℵβ : β < α⟩ 在 ℵα 中共尾，所以 cf(κ) = cf(α) ≤ α。
Regularity gives κ = cf(κ) ≤ α ≤ ℵα = κ. Hence α = κ, i.e. κ = ℵκ. ∎由正则性，κ = cf(κ) ≤ α ≤ ℵα = κ。故 α = κ，即 κ = ℵκ。∎
⚠ Common pitfall常见误区
The converse is false. The least fixed point κ₀ = ℵκ₀ is the limit of ω, ℵω, ℵℵω, …, an ω-sequence. So cf(κ₀) = ω and κ₀ is singular. Also, “strong limit” is a statement about the power set function, which ZFC leaves wide open: ℵω is a strong limit under GCH but not if 2ℵ₀ = ℵω+1, and both are consistent with ZFC. 逆命题不成立。最小不动点 κ₀ = ℵκ₀ 是 ω、ℵω、ℵℵω、…… 这个 ω 序列的极限，所以 cf(κ₀) = ω，κ₀ 是奇异基数。另外，“强极限”涉及幂集函数，而 ZFC 对幂集函数几乎没有约束：在 GCH 下 ℵω 是强极限，但若 2ℵ₀ = ℵω+1 则不是，两种情形都与 ZFC 相容。
Step 4

κ inaccessible ⇒ Vκ ⊨ ZFC, so ZFC ⊬ “an inaccessible exists”κ 不可达 ⇒ Vκ ⊨ ZFC，所以 ZFC ⊬ “存在不可达基数”

Prerequisites:预备： L14 Vα as modelsVα 作为模型 L12 Gödel 2哥德尔第二定理 Step 3第 3 步

Core idea: cut the universe at height κ. If κ is inaccessible, what remains is a whole universe of ZFC.核心想法：在高度 κ 处把宇宙截断。若 κ 不可达，剩下的部分本身就是一个完整的 ZFC 宇宙。

Choose where to cut. Each axiom tile turns green or red. Click a tile to see why.选择在哪里截断，每条公理会变绿或变红。点击公理查看原因。

Theorem (ZFC). If κ is (strongly) inaccessible, then (Vκ, ∈) ⊨ ZFC. A cardinal κ with Vκ ⊨ ZFC is called worldly. 定理（ZFC）. 若 κ（强）不可达，则 (Vκ, ∈) ⊨ ZFC。满足 Vκ ⊨ ZFC 的基数 κ 称为 worldly 基数（世俗基数）。

Proof sketch证明概要

Lemma. |Vα| < κ for all α < κ. Induction: |Vα+1| = 2|Vα| < κ because κ is a strong limit; at limit λ < κ, |Vλ| ≤ Σβ<λ|Vβ| < κ because κ is regular (fewer than κ cardinals below κ have supremum below κ). 引理. 对所有 α < κ 有 |Vα| < κ。归纳：|Vα+1| = 2|Vα| < κ，因为 κ 是强极限；在极限 λ < κ 处，|Vλ| ≤ Σβ<λ|Vβ| < κ，因为 κ 正则（少于 κ 个小于 κ 的基数，上确界仍小于 κ）。
Key closure. For x ⊆ Vκ: x ∈ Vκ ⇔ |x| < κ. (⇒ by the lemma. ⇐: the ranks of the < κ elements of x are bounded below κ by regularity, say by β, so x ∈ Vβ+1.) 关键封闭性. 对 x ⊆ Vκ：x ∈ Vκ ⇔ |x| < κ。（⇒ 由引理。⇐：x 的少于 κ 个元素的秩，由正则性有一个小于 κ 的上界 β，故 x ∈ Vβ+1。）
Easy axioms. Vκ is transitive, so Extensionality and Foundation hold. Since κ is a limit ordinal, Pairing, Union, Power Set hold (P(x) ∈ Vα+2 if x ∈ Vα). Infinity holds since ω < κ. Separation: a subset of x ∈ Vα lies in Vα+1. Choice: a well-order of x ∈ Vα lies in Vα+3. 容易的公理. Vκ 是传递集，所以外延公理、正则（基础）公理成立。κ 是极限序数，所以配对、并集、幂集公理成立（x ∈ Vα 时 P(x) ∈ Vα+2）。ω < κ，故无穷公理成立。分离公理：x ∈ Vα 的子集都在 Vα+1 中。选择公理：x ∈ Vα 的良序在 Vα+3 中。
Replacement (the heart). Let F be a class function definable over Vκ (with parameters) and x ∈ Vκ. Then F[x] ⊆ Vκ and |F[x]| ≤ |x| < κ, so F[x] ∈ Vκ by the key closure. Regularity is used here. ∎ 替换公理（核心）. 设 F 是在 Vκ 上（带参数）可定义的类函数，x ∈ Vκ。则 F[x] ⊆ Vκ 且 |F[x]| ≤ |x| < κ，由关键封闭性 F[x] ∈ Vκ。正则性正用在这里。∎
Corollary. If ZFC is consistent, then ZFC ⊬ ∃κ (κ inaccessible).推论. 若 ZFC 一致，则 ZFC ⊬ ∃κ（κ 不可达）。

Proof, with the careful Gödel 2 argument证明（仔细使用哥德尔第二定理）

The theorem above is provable in ZFC: ZFC ⊢ “if κ is inaccessible then Vκ ⊨ ZFC”. A set model gives consistency (soundness, formalized in ZFC). So ZFC + “∃ inaccessible” ⊢ Con(ZFC). 上面的定理可以在 ZFC 内证明：ZFC ⊢ “若 κ 不可达，则 Vκ ⊨ ZFC”。有集合模型就有一致性（可靠性定理可在 ZFC 中形式化）。所以 ZFC + “存在不可达” ⊢ Con(ZFC)。
Suppose ZFC ⊢ “∃ inaccessible”. Then ZFC ⊢ Con(ZFC). By Gödel's second incompleteness theorem, ZFC is inconsistent. Contrapositive: if Con(ZFC), then ZFC ⊬ “∃ inaccessible”. 假设 ZFC ⊢ “存在不可达”，则 ZFC ⊢ Con(ZFC)。由哥德尔第二不完全性定理，ZFC 不一致。逆否：若 Con(ZFC)，则 ZFC ⊬ “存在不可达”。
The other direction: take the least inaccessible. Work in a model of ZFC. If it has no inaccessible, it already satisfies “no inaccessibles”. Otherwise let κ be the least inaccessible. For λ < κ, “λ is inaccessible” means the same in Vκ as in V, because Vκ contains every subset of Vλ and every function from an ordinal < κ into λ. So Vκ ⊨ ZFC + “there is no inaccessible”. Hence Con(ZFC) ⇒ Con(ZFC + no inaccessibles). 另一方向：取最小的不可达基数. 在 ZFC 的一个模型里讨论。若其中没有不可达基数，它已满足“不存在不可达”。否则取最小的不可达基数 κ。对 λ < κ，“λ 不可达”在 Vκ 与 V 中意思相同，因为 Vκ 包含 Vλ 的所有子集，以及从小于 κ 的序数到 λ 的所有函数。所以 Vκ ⊨ ZFC + “不存在不可达基数”。因此 Con(ZFC) ⇒ Con(ZFC + 不存在不可达)。
Upshot. “∃ inaccessible” is independent of ZFC if it is consistent with ZFC. That last condition cannot be proved in ZFC: if ZFC + ∃ inaccessible is consistent, then ZFC cannot prove Con(ZFC) ⇒ Con(ZFC + ∃ inaccessible), because ZFC + ∃ inaccessible (which proves Con(ZFC)) would then prove its own consistency. 结论. 若 “存在不可达” 与 ZFC 一致，那么它独立于 ZFC。而这个前提本身在 ZFC 里证明不了：若 ZFC + 存在不可达 是一致的，则 ZFC 无法证明 Con(ZFC) ⇒ Con(ZFC + 存在不可达)，否则 ZFC + 存在不可达（它能证明 Con(ZFC)）就能证明自身的一致性。
⚠ Common pitfall常见误区
Worldly ≠ inaccessible. Every inaccessible is worldly, but the least worldly cardinal has cofinality ω (Kanamori, Lemma 6.1), so it is singular. Vκ ⊨ ZFC needs only that Replacement holds for definable functions, not for all functions. Conversely a weakly inaccessible κ with 2ℵ₀ > κ is not worldly. worldly ≠ 不可达。每个不可达基数都是 worldly 的，但最小的 worldly 基数共尾性为 ω（Kanamori 引理 6.1），是奇异的。Vκ ⊨ ZFC 只要求替换公理对可定义函数成立，而不是对所有函数。反过来，满足 2ℵ₀ > κ 的弱不可达 κ 不是 worldly 的。
Step 5

Inaccessibility goes down to L不可达性下降到 L

Prerequisites:预备： L15 L and GCHL 与 GCH Step 3第 3 步

Core idea: L has fewer functions, so it sees fewer ways to reach κ. Being “unreachable” only gets easier inside L.核心想法：L 里的函数更少，能用来够着 κ 的手段也更少。到了 L 里，“够不着”只会更容易成立。

Press the button. Each property of κ travels from V into L. Then switch on the “0#” scenario to see that the trip is one-way. 按下按钮，κ 的每条性质会从 V 传到 L。然后打开“0#”情景，看看这趟路为什么只能单向走。

Theorem (ZF). If κ is weakly inaccessible, then L ⊨ “κ is strongly inaccessible”, and Lκ = (Vκ)L ⊨ ZFC. Corollary: ZFC + “∃ weakly inaccessible” and ZFC + “∃ strongly inaccessible” are equiconsistent. 定理（ZF）. 若 κ 弱不可达，则 L ⊨ “κ 强不可达”，且 Lκ = (Vκ)L ⊨ ZFC。推论：ZFC + “存在弱不可达” 与 ZFC + “存在强不可达” 等相容。

Proof证明

Regular goes down. If L had a cofinal map f: β → κ with β < κ, then f ∈ V would witness cf(κ) < κ in V. So κ is regular in L.正则性下传. 若 L 中有共尾映射 f: β → κ（β < κ），那么 f ∈ V 就在 V 中证明了 cf(κ) < κ。所以 κ 在 L 中正则。
Limit goes down. Every cardinal of V is a cardinal of L (a bijection in L would be one in V). For λ < κ, (λ⁺)L ≤ λ⁺ < κ. So κ is a limit cardinal in L.极限性下传. V 的基数都是 L 的基数（L 中的双射也是 V 中的双射）。对 λ < κ，有 (λ⁺)L ≤ λ⁺ < κ。所以 κ 在 L 中是极限基数。
Strong limit in L. L ⊨ GCH, so in L, 2λ = (λ⁺)L < κ. Hence L ⊨ “κ is strongly inaccessible”, and by Step 4 applied inside L, (Vκ)L = Lκ ⊨ ZFC. ∎在 L 中是强极限. L ⊨ GCH，所以在 L 中 2λ = (λ⁺)L < κ。于是 L ⊨ “κ 强不可达”。在 L 内部应用第 4 步，得 (Vκ)L = Lκ ⊨ ZFC。∎
⚠ Common pitfall常见误区
The arrow only goes down. If 0# exists, every uncountable cardinal of V, for instance ℵ₁, is inaccessible in L (Silver), although ℵ₁ is certainly not inaccessible in V. And not every large cardinal property goes down to L: a measurable cardinal is never measurable in L (Scott; Lesson 24). 箭头只能朝下。若 0# 存在，V 的每个不可数基数（例如 ℵ₁）在 L 中都是不可达的（Silver），尽管 ℵ₁ 在 V 中显然不是不可达的。而且并非所有大基数性质都能下传到 L：可测基数在 L 中从来都不是可测的（Scott；见第 24 课）。
Step 6

Club sets and stationary sets无界闭集（club）与平稳集

Prerequisites:预备： L8 limit ordinals极限序数 L9 cofinality共尾性

Core idea: a club is a “large” subset of κ. Any two clubs meet, because we can zip between them. A stationary set is one that no club can avoid.核心想法：club 是 κ 的“大”子集。任意两个 club 必相交，因为可以在它们之间来回“拉拉链”。平稳集则是任何 club 都躲不开的集合。

Definitions (κ regular uncountable). C ⊆ κ is
• closed if for every limit α < κ: sup(C ∩ α) = α ⇒ α ∈ C (C contains every limit point below κ of its elements);
• unbounded if ∀β < κ ∃γ ∈ C, γ > β;
• club if both. S ⊆ κ is stationary if S ∩ C ≠ ∅ for every club C ⊆ κ.
Facts. The intersection of fewer than κ clubs is club. Every club is stationary; every stationary set is unbounded; stationary ∩ club is stationary. Examples: the limit ordinals below κ form a club; for regular λ < κ, Sκλ = {α < κ : cf α = λ} is stationary.
Fodor's lemma (pressing-down). If S ⊆ κ is stationary and f: S → κ is regressive (f(α) < α for all α ∈ S, α ≠ 0), then f is constant on some stationary S₀ ⊆ S. 定义（κ 为正则不可数基数）。C ⊆ κ 称为
• 闭的：对每个极限序数 α < κ，sup(C ∩ α) = α ⇒ α ∈ C（C 包含自身元素在 κ 以下的所有极限点）；
• 无界的：∀β < κ ∃γ ∈ C，γ > β；
• 二者兼备称为无界闭集（club）。若 S ⊆ κ 与每个 club 都相交，称 S 为平稳集。
事实. 少于 κ 个 club 的交仍是 club。club 都是平稳集；平稳集都无界；平稳集 ∩ club 仍平稳。例子：κ 以下的极限序数构成 club；对正则 λ < κ，Sκλ = {α < κ : cf α = λ} 是平稳集。
Fodor 引理（压缩引理）. 若 S ⊆ κ 平稳，f: S → κ 是退缩函数（对 α ∈ S、α ≠ 0 有 f(α) < α），则 f 在某个平稳子集 S₀ ⊆ S 上取常值。

The zipper. Blue ticks are a club C (above the line), red ticks a club D (below). Press “Zip”: we alternately pick c₀ < d₀ < c₁ < d₁ < … from C and D. The sequence has length ω < cf κ, so its supremum γ is below κ. γ is a limit point of C and of D, so by closedness γ ∈ C ∩ D. Now flip to κ = ℵω, where cf κ = ω. 拉拉链. 线上方蓝色刻度是 club C，下方红色刻度是 club D。按“拉链”：轮流从 C、D 中取 c₀ < d₀ < c₁ < d₁ < …。这个序列长度为 ω < cf κ，所以上确界 γ 落在 κ 以下。γ 是 C 的极限点也是 D 的极限点，由闭性 γ ∈ C ∩ D。再切换到 κ = ℵω 试试，那里 cf κ = ω。

Intuition. Clubs behave like “measure-one” sets and stationary sets like “positive-measure” sets. This is only an analogy: the club sets generate a filter, but two stationary sets can be disjoint (e.g. Sω₂ω and Sω₂ω₁). 直观. club 像“测度为 1”的集合，平稳集像“测度为正”的集合。这只是类比：club 生成一个滤子，但两个平稳集可以不相交（例如 Sω₂ω 与 Sω₂ω₁）。
⚠ Common pitfall常见误区
“Unbounded” is not enough. The successor ordinals below κ are unbounded but not stationary: they miss the club of limit ordinals. Also, everything here needs cf κ > ω. In ℵω, {ℵ2n} and {ℵ2n+1} are disjoint clubs. “无界”还不够。κ 以下的后继序数无界，但不平稳：它们与“极限序数”这个 club 不相交。另外，以上一切都需要 cf κ > ω。在 ℵω 中，{ℵ2n} 与 {ℵ2n+1} 是两个不相交的 club。
Step 7

The inaccessible hierarchy and Mahlo cardinals不可达层级与马洛基数

Prerequisites:预备： Step 3第 3 步 Step 6第 6 步

Core idea: “unboundedly many inaccessibles below” can be iterated forever. Mahlo means something stronger: the inaccessibles below κ are stationary, so no club can dodge them.核心想法：“下方有无界多个不可达”这件事可以无限迭代下去。马洛要求更多：κ 以下的不可达基数构成平稳集，任何 club 都躲不开它们。

Definitions.
• κ is α-inaccessible if κ is inaccessible and for every β < α the β-inaccessibles below κ are unbounded in κ. (0-inaccessible = inaccessible; 1-inaccessible = inaccessible limit of inaccessibles. Equivalently, letting ψ₀ enumerate the inaccessibles, the 1-inaccessibles are the inaccessible fixed points κ = ψ₀(κ).)
• Hyper-inaccessible is used ambiguously: most often for 1-inaccessible, sometimes for κ-inaccessible, occasionally for Mahlo. Always check the author's definition.
• κ is (strongly) Mahlo if κ is inaccessible and {λ < κ : λ inaccessible} is stationary in κ. Weakly Mahlo: weakly inaccessible, and the weakly inaccessibles below κ are stationary. 定义.
• 若 κ 不可达，且对每个 β < α，κ 以下的 β-不可达基数在 κ 中无界，称 κ 为 α-不可达。（0-不可达 = 不可达；1-不可达 = 不可达基数的不可达极限。等价地，令 ψ₀ 枚举所有不可达基数，则 1-不可达就是满足 κ = ψ₀(κ) 的不可达不动点。）
• 超不可达（hyper-inaccessible）一词用法不统一：最常指 1-不可达，有时指 κ-不可达，偶尔指马洛。务必看清作者的定义。
• 若 κ 不可达，且 {λ < κ : λ 不可达} 在 κ 中平稳，称 κ 为（强）马洛基数。弱马洛：κ 弱不可达，且 κ 以下的弱不可达基数构成平稳集。

Gold diamonds are inaccessibles below κ; blue circles are limits of inaccessibles. Compare the two scenarios.金色菱形是 κ 以下的不可达基数，蓝色圆圈是不可达基数的极限点。对比两种情景。

The picture is schematic. In reality there are κ many points, and a club has order type κ.图是示意性的：实际上有 κ 个点，club 的序型是 κ。

Theorem. If κ is Mahlo, then κ is α-inaccessible for every α ≤ κ (so κ is the κ-th inaccessible, the κ-th 1-inaccessible, …), and for each α < κ the α-inaccessibles below κ form a stationary set. Hence the least Mahlo cardinal is far above the least hyper-inaccessible in every sense of that word. Consistency: if κ is Mahlo, Vκ ⊨ ZFC + “there is a proper class of inaccessibles”, so ZFC + ∃Mahlo ⊢ Con(ZFC + ∃ inaccessible). 定理. 若 κ 是马洛基数，则对每个 α ≤ κ，κ 都是 α-不可达的（所以 κ 是第 κ 个不可达基数、第 κ 个 1-不可达基数……），并且对每个 α < κ，κ 以下的 α-不可达基数构成平稳集。因此，无论“超不可达”取哪种含义，最小马洛基数都远在最小超不可达基数之上。一致性方面：若 κ 是马洛基数，则 Vκ ⊨ ZFC + “不可达基数构成真类”，所以 ZFC + 存在马洛 ⊢ Con(ZFC + 存在不可达)。

Proof sketch (first two levels; the rest iterates)证明概要（前两层，其余同理迭代）

I = {λ < κ : λ inaccessible} is stationary, hence unbounded. Since κ is regular, I has order type κ: κ is 1-inaccessible and κ = ψ₀(κ).I = {λ < κ : λ 不可达} 平稳，从而无界。κ 正则，所以 I 的序型是 κ：κ 是 1-不可达的，且 κ = ψ₀(κ)。
L = {λ < κ : λ is a limit of inaccessibles} is club: a limit of limits is a limit (closed), and above any β we can take the sup of ω inaccessibles > β, which stays below κ by regularity (unbounded).L = {λ < κ : λ 是不可达基数的极限} 是 club：极限点的极限仍是极限点（闭）；对任意 β，取 β 以上 ω 个不可达基数的上确界，由正则性仍小于 κ（无界）。
I ∩ L = the 1-inaccessibles below κ, and stationary ∩ club is stationary. So the 1-inaccessibles below κ are stationary, in particular unbounded: κ is 2-inaccessible. Repeat with “limit of β-inaccessibles” for all β; at limit stages intersect fewer than κ clubs. ∎I ∩ L 恰是 κ 以下的 1-不可达基数，而平稳集 ∩ club 仍平稳。所以 κ 以下的 1-不可达基数平稳，特别地无界：κ 是 2-不可达的。对所有 β 用“β-不可达基数的极限”重复此论证；在极限阶段取少于 κ 个 club 的交。∎
⚠ Common pitfall常见误区
Mahlo is not “just one more fixed point”. Scenario A shows an inaccessible limit of inaccessibles that is not Mahlo: the club of limits of inaccessibles below it contains no inaccessible at all. Stationarity is a qualitatively stronger demand than unboundedness. 马洛不是“再多一个不动点”而已。情景 A 中的 κ 是不可达基数的不可达极限，却不是马洛基数：它下方“不可达基数的极限点”构成的 club 里一个不可达基数都没有。平稳性比无界性在质上要求更高。
Step 8

Weakly compact I: the partition property κ → (κ)²₂弱紧（一）：划分性质 κ → (κ)²₂

Prerequisites:预备： Step 3第 3 步 L9 cardinals基数

Core idea: color every pair. Can you always find a big set whose pairs all have the same color? For finite graphs and for ω, yes (Ramsey). For uncountable κ, only if κ is a large cardinal.核心想法：给每一对元素染色。总能找到一个大集合，使其中所有的对同色吗？对有限图和 ω：能（拉姆齐）。对不可数的 κ：只有 κ 是大基数时才能。

Click an edge to cycle its color (grey → red → blue). Monochromatic triangles light up. On K₆ you can never avoid one. Press “Pigeonhole proof” to see why. 点击边切换颜色（灰 → 红 → 蓝），同色三角形会被点亮。在 K₆ 上你永远避免不了同色三角形。按“鸽笼证明”看原因。

Notation. [X]² = the set of 2-element subsets of X. κ → (κ)²₂ means: for every f: [κ]² → {0,1} there is H ⊆ κ with |H| = κ such that f is constant on [H]² (H is homogeneous).
Ramsey's theorem. ω → (ω)²₂. Finite version: R(3,3) = 6.
Definition. An uncountable cardinal κ is weakly compact if κ → (κ)²₂.
Facts. (Erdős–Tarski) if κ is uncountable and κ → (κ)²₂, then κ is inaccessible. (Sierpiński, ZFC) 2ℵ₀ ↛ (ℵ₁)²₂; in particular ω₁ ↛ (ω₁)²₂. 记号. [X]² 表示 X 的所有二元子集。κ → (κ)²₂ 的意思是：对任意 f: [κ]² → {0,1}，存在 H ⊆ κ，|H| = κ，使 f 在 [H]² 上为常值（H 是齐性集）。
拉姆齐定理. ω → (ω)²₂。有限版本：R(3,3) = 6。
定义. 满足 κ → (κ)²₂ 的不可数基数 κ 称为弱紧基数。
事实.（Erdős–Tarski）若 κ 不可数且 κ → (κ)²₂，则 κ 不可达。（Sierpiński，ZFC）2ℵ₀ ↛ (ℵ₁)²₂，特别地 ω₁ ↛ (ω₁)²₂。

Proof of Ramsey's theorem ω → (ω)²₂拉姆齐定理 ω → (ω)²₂ 的证明

Let A₀ = ω and a₀ = min A₀. Infinitely many b ∈ A₀ ∖ {a₀} share the same color f({a₀, b}) =: c₀ (pigeonhole with 2 colors). Let A₁ be that infinite set.令 A₀ = ω，a₀ = min A₀。A₀ ∖ {a₀} 中有无穷多个 b 使 f({a₀, b}) 取同一颜色 c₀（两色鸽笼原理）。记这个无穷集为 A₁。
Repeat: an = min An, and An+1 ⊆ An ∖ {an} is infinite with f({an, b}) = cn for all b ∈ An+1. Then for m > n, f({an, am}) = cn: the color depends only on the smaller point.重复：an = min An，取无穷集 An+1 ⊆ An ∖ {an}，使对所有 b ∈ An+1 有 f({an, b}) = cn。于是对 m > n，f({an, am}) = cn：颜色只取决于较小的那个点。
Some color c occurs as cn for infinitely many n. H = {an : cn = c} is infinite and homogeneous. ∎
For uncountable κ this cascade breaks at limit stages: a decreasing sequence of large sets An can have empty intersection. At ω₁ the failure is unavoidable (Sierpiński). At a weakly compact κ one repairs it by organizing all the choices into a κ-tree and taking a cofinal branch (the tree property, Step 9).某个颜色 c 作为 cn 出现无穷多次。H = {an : cn = c} 是无穷齐性集。∎
对不可数的 κ，这个级联会在极限步断掉：一列递减的大集合 An 的交可能是空集。在 ω₁ 处这种失败无法避免（Sierpiński）。在弱紧基数 κ 处，可以把所有选择组织成一棵 κ-树，再取它的共尾枝来补救（树性质，第 9 步）。
⚠ Common pitfall常见误区
ω → (ω)²₂ does not make ω a “weakly compact cardinal”: the definition requires κ uncountable, exactly as with inaccessibility. And Ramsey's theorem does not generalize to ω₁, despite how natural it looks. ω → (ω)²₂ 并不使 ω 成为“弱紧基数”：和不可达一样，定义要求 κ 不可数。并且拉姆齐定理不能推广到 ω₁，尽管看起来很自然。
Step 9

Weakly compact II: the tree property and compactness弱紧（二）：树性质与紧致性

Prerequisites:预备： L5 compactness紧致性 Step 8第 8 步

Core idea: a tall tree with small levels must have a branch all the way up. True at ω (Kőnig), false at ω₁ (Aronszajn), true at weakly compact κ.核心想法：又高、每层又小的树，必定有一条通到顶的枝。在 ω 处成立（柯尼希），在 ω₁ 处不成立（阿龙扎扬），在弱紧基数处成立。

Definitions. A tree is a partial order (T, <T) in which the predecessors of each node are well-ordered. Level α = the nodes whose predecessors have order type α. A κ-tree has height κ and every level of size < κ. A cofinal branch is a linearly ordered subset meeting every level. κ has the tree property if every κ-tree has a cofinal branch. A κ-tree with no cofinal branch is a κ-Aronszajn tree. 定义. 树是一个偏序 (T, <T)，其中每个节点的前驱集都是良序的。第 α 层 = 前驱序型为 α 的节点。κ-树高度为 κ 且每层大小 < κ。共尾枝是与每一层都相交的线序子集。若每棵 κ-树都有共尾枝，称 κ 具有树性质。没有共尾枝的 κ-树称为 κ-阿龙扎扬树。

Kőnig's walk. In a finite picture, “has infinitely many descendants” is played by “reaches the bottom row”. Click children to walk down from the root. Green nodes are alive, grey ones are dead ends. Kőnig's rule: always step to an alive child. One exists because finitely many children cannot all have finitely many descendants. 柯尼希漫步. 在有限的图里，用“能到达最底层”来扮演“有无穷多个后代”。从根开始点击子节点往下走。绿色节点是活的，灰色节点是死胡同。柯尼希的规则：每一步都走向一个活的子节点。这样的子节点一定存在，因为有限多个子节点不可能每个都只有有限多个后代。

Theorem (equivalent definitions). For an uncountable cardinal κ the following are equivalent:
(a) κ → (κ)²₂ (Step 8);
(b) κ is inaccessible and has the tree property;
(c) κ is Π¹₁-indescribable (Step 10);
(d) κ is inaccessible and Lκ,κ satisfies the weak compactness theorem: if Σ is a set of at most κ sentences of Lκ,κ (conjunctions of < κ formulas, blocks of < κ quantifiers) and every subset of Σ of size < κ has a model, then Σ has a model.
Kőnig: ω has the tree property. Aronszajn (ZFC): there is an ω₁-Aronszajn tree, so ω₁ lacks it. 定理（等价定义）. 对不可数基数 κ，以下等价：
(a) κ → (κ)²₂（第 8 步）；
(b) κ 不可达且具有树性质；
(c) κ 是 Π¹₁-不可描述的（第 10 步）；
(d) κ 不可达，且 Lκ,κ 满足弱紧致性定理：若 Σ 是至多 κ 个 Lκ,κ 语句（允许少于 κ 个公式的合取、少于 κ 个量词的量词块）构成的集合，且 Σ 的每个大小 < κ 的子集都有模型，则 Σ 有模型。
柯尼希：ω 有树性质。阿龙扎扬（ZFC）：存在 ω₁-阿龙扎扬树，所以 ω₁ 没有树性质。
Why “weakly compact”? Property (d) is the infinitary analogue of the compactness theorem from Lesson 5 (where κ = ω and Lω,ω is first-order logic). “Weakly” refers to the restriction |Σ| ≤ κ; dropping it gives the much stronger strongly compact cardinals (Lesson 25). 为什么叫“弱紧”？ 性质 (d) 是第 5 课紧致性定理的无穷语言版本（那里 κ = ω，Lω,ω 就是一阶逻辑）。“弱”指限制 |Σ| ≤ κ；去掉这个限制，就得到强得多的强紧基数（第 25 课）。
⚠ Common pitfall常见误区
The tree property alone does not make κ large in size. It is consistent (relative to a weakly compact, Mitchell) that ℵ₂ has the tree property, and ℵ₂ is a successor cardinal. Conversely, if ℵ₂ has the tree property then ℵ₂ is weakly compact in L (Silver). So “ℵ₂ has the tree property” is equiconsistent with “∃ weakly compact”, yet ℵ₂ is not weakly compact. Inaccessibility in (b) cannot be dropped. 树性质本身并不能让 κ 在大小上“大”。（相对于弱紧基数，Mitchell）“ℵ₂ 有树性质”是一致的，而 ℵ₂ 是后继基数。反过来，若 ℵ₂ 有树性质，则 ℵ₂ 在 L 中弱紧（Silver）。所以“ℵ₂ 有树性质”与“存在弱紧基数”等相容，但 ℵ₂ 并不是弱紧基数。(b) 中的不可达条件不能去掉。
Step 10

Indescribable cardinals: reflection internalized不可描述基数：内化的反射

Prerequisites:预备： L20 reflection theorem反射定理 Step 7第 7 步 Step 9第 9 步

Core idea: whatever Vκ says about itself (in a given language) is already said by some smaller Vα. So no sentence of that language can single κ out.核心想法：Vκ 关于自身说的任何话（在给定语言中），某个更小的 Vα 早已说过。因此该语言中没有任何语句能把 κ 单独挑出来。

Definition (Kanamori's convention). Πmn formulas: (m+1)-th-order formulas whose highest-order quantifiers form a Πn prefix (n alternating blocks, beginning with ∀). For m = 1 these are second-order: the quantified variables range over subsets of Vκ. κ is Πmn-indescribable if for every R ⊆ Vκ and every Πmn sentence φ:
⟨Vκ, ∈, R⟩ ⊨ φ ⇒ ∃α < κ ⟨Vα, ∈, R ∩ Vα⟩ ⊨ φ.
Totally indescribable: Πmn-indescribable for all m, n.
Facts. κ inaccessible ⇔ first-order reflection with a predicate holds at κ (Π0n-indescribable for all n). κ weakly compact ⇔ Π¹₁-indescribable (Hanf–Scott). If κ is Π¹n+1-indescribable, the Π¹n-indescribables below κ form a stationary set. 定义（采用 Kanamori 的记号）。Πmn 公式：(m+1) 阶公式，其最高阶量词构成 Πn 前缀（n 个交替的量词块，以 ∀ 开头）。m = 1 时就是二阶公式：被量化的变元取遍 Vκ 的子集。若对每个 R ⊆ Vκ 和每个 Πmn 语句 φ 都有
⟨Vκ, ∈, R⟩ ⊨ φ ⇒ ∃α < κ ⟨Vα, ∈, R ∩ Vα⟩ ⊨ φ，
则称 κ 是 Πmn-不可描述的。对所有 m、n 都成立，称为完全不可描述。
事实. κ 不可达 ⇔ 带谓词的一阶反射在 κ 处成立（对所有 n 都 Π0n-不可描述）。κ 弱紧 ⇔ Π¹₁-不可描述（Hanf–Scott）。若 κ 是 Π¹n+1-不可描述的，则 κ 以下的 Π¹n-不可描述基数构成平稳集。

The reflection machine. Pick a cardinal κ and a sentence about “the ordinals” of Vκ (which are exactly the ordinals below κ). The machine checks whether it holds in Vκ, then looks for a smaller α that also satisfies it. 反射机. 选一个基数 κ 和一条关于 Vκ 的“序数”（恰为 κ 以下的序数）的语句。机器先检查它在 Vκ 中是否成立，再去找一个同样满足它的更小的 α。

Reflection internalized. The ZFC reflection theorem (L20) says: for each first-order φ true in V, there are (club-many) α with Vα ⊨ φ. It is a schema about the whole universe, with no top. Indescribability moves this to a set-sized level κ and upgrades it to higher-order φ with a predicate R. ZFC proves the first-order schema for V; it cannot prove that some κ has second-order (Π¹₁) reflection, since such a κ is weakly compact, hence inaccessible. 内化的反射. ZFC 的反射定理（第 20 课）说：对每个在 V 中成立的一阶语句 φ，存在（club 多个）α 使 Vα ⊨ φ。那是关于整个宇宙的定理模式，没有“顶”。不可描述性把它搬到一个集合大小的高度 κ，并升级为带谓词 R 的高阶语句。ZFC 能证明关于 V 的一阶模式，却证明不了存在具有二阶（Π¹₁）反射的 κ，因为这样的 κ 是弱紧的，从而不可达。
Theorem (ZFC). Every weakly compact cardinal κ is Mahlo, and the Mahlo cardinals below κ form a stationary subset of κ.定理（ZFC）. 每个弱紧基数 κ 都是马洛基数，并且 κ 以下的马洛基数构成 κ 的平稳子集。

Proof via Π¹₁ reflection用 Π¹₁ 反射证明

Let C ⊆ κ be club; we find an inaccessible in C. Use the predicate R = C. In ⟨Vκ, ∈, C⟩ the sentence φ = “Ord is uncountable and a strong limit, and C is unbounded in Ord, and ∀X (if X is a function from an ordinal into Ord then ran X is bounded)” is true, because κ is inaccessible. Only the ∀X clause is second-order, so φ is Π¹₁.设 C ⊆ κ 是 club，我们要在 C 中找一个不可达基数。取谓词 R = C。在 ⟨Vκ, ∈, C⟩ 中，语句 φ = “Ord 不可数且是强极限，C 在 Ord 中无界，并且 ∀X（若 X 是从某序数到 Ord 的函数，则 ran X 有界）” 成立，因为 κ 不可达。只有 ∀X 一句是二阶的，所以 φ 是 Π¹₁ 语句。
By Π¹₁-indescribability there is α < κ with ⟨Vα, ∈, C ∩ α⟩ ⊨ φ. Second-order quantifiers over Vα range over all subsets of Vα, so α really is regular, uncountable, and a strong limit: α is inaccessible.由 Π¹₁-不可描述性，存在 α < κ 使 ⟨Vα, ∈, C ∩ α⟩ ⊨ φ。Vα 上的二阶量词取遍 Vα 的所有子集，所以 α 确实正则、不可数且是强极限：α 不可达。
Also C ∩ α is unbounded in α, and C is closed, so α ∈ C. Thus every club meets the inaccessibles: κ is Mahlo.并且 C ∩ α 在 α 中无界，而 C 是闭的，所以 α ∈ C。于是每个 club 都碰到不可达基数：κ 是马洛基数。
Now “Ord is Mahlo” = “… ∧ ∀X (X club in Ord → X contains an inaccessible)” is also Π¹₁ and true in Vκ. Add “C is unbounded” and reflect again: we get a Mahlo α ∈ C. So the Mahlo cardinals below κ are stationary. ∎而“Ord 是马洛的” = “… ∧ ∀X（X 是 Ord 中的 club → X 含一个不可达基数）” 也是 Π¹₁ 语句，且在 Vκ 中成立。加上“C 无界”再反射一次，就得到一个马洛基数 α ∈ C。所以 κ 以下的马洛基数平稳。∎
⚠ Common pitfall常见误区
The parameter R matters, and so does the language. The least weakly compact κ is Π¹₁-indescribable but not Π¹₂-indescribable: “Ord is weakly compact” is a Π¹₂ sentence (∀ tree ∃ branch) true in Vκ and false in every Vα, α < κ. Try it in the machine. 参数 R 很重要，语言的复杂度也很重要。最小的弱紧基数 κ 是 Π¹₁-不可描述的，但不是 Π¹₂-不可描述的：“Ord 是弱紧的”是一条 Π¹₂ 语句（∀ 树 ∃ 枝），在 Vκ 中成立，在每个 Vα（α < κ）中都不成立。在反射机里试一试。

Lesson 24: measurable cardinals & elementary embeddings →第 24 课：可测基数与初等嵌入 →

Everything so far lives “below” L: inaccessible, Mahlo and weakly compact cardinals all remain so in L. The next rung, the measurable cardinal, is different: it is witnessed by a nonprincipal κ-complete ultrafilter on κ, equivalently by an elementary embedding j: V → M with critical point κ, and it implies V ≠ L. After that, Lesson 25 climbs to Woodin, supercompact, huge and rank-into-rank cardinals, and their link to determinacy. 目前为止的一切都在 L 的“下面”：不可达、马洛、弱紧基数在 L 中依然保持原样。下一级可测基数则不同：它由 κ 上的一个非主 κ-完全超滤子见证，等价地由一个临界点为 κ 的初等嵌入 j: V → M 见证，并且它蕴涵 V ≠ L。之后第 25 课将继续爬向伍丁基数、超紧基数、巨大基数、秩入秩基数，以及它们与决定性的联系。
