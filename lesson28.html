§1 · Model theory模型论

From Morley to Shelah: a map of the universe of theories从莫利到谢拉赫：理论宇宙的地图

Prerequisites前置: L3 · structures结构 L5 · compactness, Löwenheim–Skolem紧致性、勒文海姆–斯科伦 L9 · cardinals基数
One idea. Shelah's classification theory looks for dividing lines: properties of a theory such that on one side there is a structure theory (dimension, independence, few models) and on the other side there is provable chaos (as many models as possible). The map below places theories you know relative to these lines. 核心思想。谢拉赫的分类理论寻找分界线：分界线一侧的理论有结构理论（维数、独立性、模型很少），另一侧则可以证明一片混乱（模型多到不能再多）。下图把你熟悉的理论放到这些分界线之间。

It started with Morley's theorem. By Löwenheim–Skolem, a theory with an infinite model has models of every infinite size, so the best one can hope for is one model per size (categoricity). Morley showed this hope is very rigid: categoricity in one uncountable size forces it in all of them. Shelah's question was: what is it about a theory that controls how many models it has? Click a region or a theory. 一切始于莫利定理。由勒文海姆–斯科伦定理，有无穷模型的理论在每个无穷基数上都有模型，所以最好的情形就是每个基数上只有一个模型（范畴性）。莫利证明这种情形非常“刚硬”：在某一个不可数基数上范畴，就在所有不可数基数上范畴。谢拉赫接着问：理论的什么性质决定了它有多少个模型？点击区域或理论试试。

Morley's categoricity theorem (Morley 1965). Let T be a complete theory in a countable language. If T is κ-categorical for some uncountable κ, then T is κ-categorical for every uncountable κ.
Dividing lines (formula φ(x; y), tuples ai, bj in a model of T):
· φ has the order property if there are ai, bj (i, j < ω) with ⊨ φ(ai, bj) ⇔ i < j. T is stable if no formula has it.
· φ has the independence property if there are ai (i < ω) and bS (S ⊆ ω) with ⊨ φ(ai, bS) ⇔ i ∈ S. T is NIP if no formula has it.
· T is simple if no formula has the tree property; NTP₂ and NSOP₁ are weaker “tree-type” conditions.
Facts (Shelah; Džamonja–Shelah; Kaplan–Ramsey; Mutchnik for the last one: arXiv 2022, J. Eur. Math. Soc. 2026): stable = NIP ∩ simple; simple ∪ NIP ⊆ NTP₂; simple ⊆ NSOP₁; NTP₂ ∩ NSOP₁ = simple; NSOP₁ = NSOP₂.
Main Gap (Shelah, completed 1980s): for countable complete T, either T has the maximum number 2λ of models in every uncountable λ, or T is superstable and satisfies further structural conditions (NDOP, NOTOP, shallow) under which every model decomposes along a well-founded tree of countable elementary submodels, and the number of models grows much more slowly. 莫利范畴性定理 （莫利，1965）。设 T 是可数语言中的完备理论。若 T 在某个不可数基数 κ 上范畴，则 T 在每个不可数基数 κ 上都范畴。
分界线（公式 φ(x; y)，ai、bj 是 T 的某个模型中的元组）：
· 若存在 ai、bj（i, j < ω）使 ⊨ φ(ai, bj) ⇔ i < j，则称 φ 有序性质。没有公式具有序性质的 T 称为稳定的。
· 若存在 ai（i < ω）和 bS（S ⊆ ω）使 ⊨ φ(ai, bS) ⇔ i ∈ S，则称 φ 有独立性质。没有公式具有独立性质的 T 称为 NIP 理论。
· 没有公式具有树性质的 T 称为简单理论；NTP₂、NSOP₁ 是更弱的“树型”条件。
事实 （谢拉赫；Džamonja–Shelah；Kaplan–Ramsey；最后一条为 Mutchnik：2022 年 arXiv，2026 年发表于 J. Eur. Math. Soc.）：稳定 = NIP ∩ 简单；简单 ∪ NIP ⊆ NTP₂；简单 ⊆ NSOP₁；NTP₂ ∩ NSOP₁ = 简单；NSOP₁ = NSOP₂。
主间隙定理 （谢拉赫，1980 年代完成）：对可数完备理论 T，要么 T 在每个不可数基数 λ 上都有最大可能数目 2λ 个模型，要么 T 是超稳定的并满足进一步的结构条件（NDOP、NOTOP、浅），此时每个模型都可以沿一棵由可数初等子模型组成的良基树分解，模型个数的增长要慢得多。
Why people care / Motivation. “Tameness ⇒ structure”: on the tame side of a line, theories come with a geometry (independence, dimension, ranks), and that geometry is a tool for other mathematics. Hrushovski's proof of the function-field Mordell–Lang conjecture (1996) used stable-theory geometry. NIP connects to learning theory (§5). o-minimality (§3), a tame part of NIP, drives recent results in number theory. Open directions : find the right structure theory for NTP₂ and NSOP₁ theories. Is NSOP₃ = NSOP₂? This was open as of 2025. In August 2026 Chernikov posted a 5-page preprint claiming a proof of SOP₂ = SOP₃ (arXiv:2608.13291), which together with Mutchnik would give NSOP₁ = NSOP₂ = NSOP₃. The paper says the proof was found with the help of ChatGPT . Beyond first-order logic there is Shelah's eventual categoricity conjecture for abstract elementary classes (as of 2025). Partial results are due to Grossberg–VanDieren, Vasey and others. A claimed ZFC proof by Espíndola (arXiv 2019, revised 2022) has not, to our knowledge, been published or generally accepted. A September 2026 AI-generated manuscript in the OpenAI release also claims a ZFC proof. Its main theorem is not Lean-formalized; only a companion result is (under CH, a counterexample to one proposed value of the threshold) . 动机 / 为什么关心。“驯顺 ⇒ 结构”：在分界线驯顺的一侧，理论自带一套几何（独立性、维数、秩），而这套几何又能成为其他数学的工具。赫鲁晓夫斯基对函数域上莫德尔–朗猜想的证明（1996）就用了稳定理论的几何。NIP 与学习理论相连（§5）。o-极小性（§3）是 NIP 中的驯顺部分，推动了近年数论的重要进展。未解决方向 ：为 NTP₂ 和 NSOP₁ 理论找到合适的结构理论。NSOP₃ 是否等于 NSOP₂？以 2025 年为准这还未解决。2026 年 8 月，Chernikov 发布了一篇 5 页的预印本，声称证明了 SOP₂ = SOP₃（arXiv:2608.13291）；结合 Mutchnik 的结果，就得到 NSOP₁ = NSOP₂ = NSOP₃。论文说明该证明是借助 ChatGPT 找到的 。超出一阶逻辑之外，有谢拉赫关于抽象初等类的最终范畴性猜想 （以 2025 年为准）。Grossberg–VanDieren、Vasey 等人有部分结果。Espíndola 声称给出了 ZFC 中的证明（arXiv 2019，2022 年修订），但据我们所知尚未发表，也未被普遍接受。2026 年 9 月，OpenAI 公布的一份 AI 生成手稿也声称给出了 ZFC 中的证明。其主定理没有 Lean 形式化；被形式化的只是一个附带结果（在 CH 下，对阈值的某个提议取值给出反例）。
⚠ Common pitfall. “Stable” here has nothing to do with stability in dynamics. Also, the map is about complete theories, not structures in isolation: (ℤ, +) is stable, but (ℤ, +, ·) is as wild as possible, even though both have the same universe. Adding one function can push you across every line. ⚠ 常见误区。这里的“稳定”和动力系统里的稳定性毫无关系。另外，地图上的点是完备理论，不是孤立的结构：(ℤ, +) 是稳定的，而 (ℤ, +, ·) 则狂野到了极点，尽管两者的论域相同。多加一个函数，就可能越过所有分界线。
§2 · Model theory模型论

Vaught's conjecture: how many countable models?沃特猜想：可数模型有多少个？

Prerequisites前置: §1 L5 · countable models可数模型 L9 · ℵ₀, ℵ₁, 2^ℵ₀ℵ₀、ℵ₁、2^ℵ₀ L16 · CH is independentCH 独立 L19 · perfect sets完美集
One idea. Count the countable models of a complete theory T (up to isomorphism); call the number I(T, ℵ₀). It can be 1, 3, 17, ℵ₀ or 2ℵ₀. Vaught conjectured that it can never be strictly between ℵ₀ and 2ℵ₀, even when the continuum is huge. 核心思想。数一数完备理论 T 有多少个互不同构的可数模型，记作 I(T, ℵ₀)。它可以是 1、3、17、ℵ₀ 或 2ℵ₀。沃特猜想：它永远不会严格介于 ℵ₀ 和 2ℵ₀ 之间，哪怕连续统非常大。

Drag the slider to choose a size of the continuum (each choice is consistent with ZFC, by forcing). Then drop example theories onto the number line. 拖动滑块选择连续统的大小（由力迫法，每种选择都与 ZFC 一致）。然后把例子理论放到数轴上。

2ℵ₀ = ℵ2
Vaught's conjecture (Vaught 1961). If T is a complete theory in a countable language, then I(T, ℵ₀) ≤ ℵ₀ or I(T, ℵ₀) = 2ℵ₀. The intended, ZFC-robust form: if T has uncountably many countable models, then it has a perfect set of pairwise non-isomorphic countable models.
Known : I(T, ℵ₀) ≠ 2 (Vaught 1961); I(T, ℵ₀) ∈ {…, ℵ₀, ℵ₁, 2ℵ₀} once it is infinite (Morley 1970), so the only possible counterexample has exactly ℵ₁ models and no perfect set of them. The conjecture holds for ω-stable theories (Shelah–Harrington–Makkai 1984), o-minimal theories (Mayer 1988) and superstable theories of finite U-rank (Buechler 2008).
Not accepted: Knight announced a first-order counterexample (2002, revised 2007). It was never generally accepted, and the first-order conjecture is still regarded as open.
Infinitary version, 2026 : in October 2026 Ackerman, Freer and Knight posted a sentence of Lω₁,ω with exactly ℵ₁ countable models and no perfect set of them (arXiv:2610.15217). The construction follows Knight's framework, and the authors report a complete Lean formalization. It has not yet been refereed. If it holds up, it refutes Vaught's conjecture for Lω₁,ω (and hence the topological Vaught conjecture below), but not the first-order conjecture stated above. 沃特猜想 （沃特，1961）。若 T 是可数语言中的完备理论，则 I(T, ℵ₀) ≤ ℵ₀ 或 I(T, ℵ₀) = 2ℵ₀。它真正想说的、在 ZFC 中稳健的形式是：若 T 有不可数多个可数模型，则 T 有一个由两两不同构的可数模型组成的完美集。
已知 ：I(T, ℵ₀) ≠ 2（沃特，1961）；一旦它是无穷的，就只能是 ℵ₀、ℵ₁ 或 2ℵ₀（莫利，1970），所以唯一可能的反例恰有 ℵ₁ 个可数模型，并且其中不含完美集。猜想对 ω-稳定理论（Shelah–Harrington–Makkai，1984）、o-极小理论（Mayer，1988）和有限 U-秩的超稳定理论（Buechler，2008）成立。
未被接受：Knight 宣布过一个一阶反例（2002，2007 年修订）。它始终未被普遍接受，学界仍视一阶猜想为未解决。
无穷逻辑版本，2026 ：2026 年 10 月，Ackerman、Freer 与 Knight 发布了一个 Lω₁,ω 句子，它恰有 ℵ₁ 个可数模型，且这些模型中不含完美集（arXiv:2610.15217）。构造沿用 Knight 的框架，作者称已在 Lean 中完整形式化。该文尚未经过审稿。若成立，它推翻 Lω₁,ω 的沃特猜想（因而也推翻下文的拓扑沃特猜想），但不推翻上面陈述的一阶猜想。
Why people care / Motivation. Counting models measures how hard it is to classify them. Below ℵ₀: a list. At 2ℵ₀ with a perfect set: like classifying all real numbers. ℵ₁ without a perfect set would be a strange intermediate complexity. The question sits exactly where model theory meets descriptive set theory (L19): the isomorphism relation on countable models is an analytic equivalence relation, and the conjecture is a special case of the topological Vaught conjecture about orbits of Polish group actions (as of 2025; the 2026 Lω₁,ω claim above would refute it). 动机 / 为什么关心。数模型，其实是在衡量分类它们有多难。不超过 ℵ₀：可以列成一张表。2ℵ₀ 并含完美集：难度相当于给所有实数分类。恰为 ℵ₁ 却没有完美集，则是一种奇怪的中间复杂度。这个问题正好位于模型论与描述集合论（第 19 课）的交界处：可数模型上的同构关系是一个解析等价关系，而该猜想是关于波兰群作用轨道的拓扑沃特猜想 （以 2025 年为准；上述 2026 年关于 Lω₁,ω 的声称若成立，将推翻它）的特例。
⚠ Common pitfall. “Under CH the conjecture is trivially true, so it is just a question about ¬CH.” Only the naive cardinal-counting form is trivial under CH. The real question (ℵ₁ models but no perfect set?) is a statement about Σ¹₁ equivalence relations, and it does not depend on CH: nobody knows the answer in any model of ZFC. ⚠ 常见误区。“CH 成立时猜想平凡为真，所以它只是 ¬CH 下的问题。”只有朴素的“数基数”形式在 CH 下才平凡。真正的问题（有 ℵ₁ 个模型却没有完美集？）是关于 Σ¹₁ 等价关系的命题，与 CH 无关：在 ZFC 的任何模型里，都没人知道答案。
§3 · Model theory模型论

o-minimality: tame geometry, and rational pointso-极小性：驯顺几何与有理点

Prerequisites前置: L3 · definable sets可定义集 L7 · ℝ, ℚℝ、ℚ L13 · Tarski塔斯基
One idea. An expansion of (ℝ, <) is o-minimal if every definable subset of the line is a finite union of points and intervals. That single condition on the line forces every definable set in ℝn to have finitely many pieces, with no fractals and no infinite oscillation. 核心思想。若 (ℝ, <) 的一个扩张中，直线上的每个可定义子集都是有限多个点与区间之并，就称它为 o-极小的。这一条只针对直线的条件，就迫使 ℝn 中每个可定义集都只有有限多块：没有分形，没有无穷振荡。

(a) Is the structure o-minimal? Look at the zero set on the line.(a) 这个结构是 o-极小的吗？看看直线上的零点集。

(b) The Pila–Wilkie counting theorem, live(b) 现场演示皮拉–威尔基计数定理

The height of a rational a/b in lowest terms is max(|a|, |b|), and a point's height is the largest height of its coordinates. Count rational points of height ≤ H on two curves: the parabola y = x² (algebraic) and y = 2x (transcendental, definable in the o-minimal ℝexp). Move H. 既约分数 a/b 的高度是 max(|a|, |b|)，点的高度取各坐标高度的最大值。分别数一数两条曲线上高度 ≤ H 的有理点：抛物线 y = x²（代数曲线）和 y = 2x（超越曲线，可在 o-极小结构 ℝexp 中定义）。拖动 H。

H = 100
Wilkie's theorem (Wilkie 1996): ℝexp = (ℝ, +, ·, exp) is model complete and o-minimal. (Earlier: semialgebraic sets, Tarski 1930s; ℝan, van den Dries 1986 via Gabrielov; later ℝan,exp, van den Dries–Macintyre–Marker 1994.)
Pila–Wilkie counting theorem (2006). Let X ⊆ ℝn be definable in an o-minimal expansion of the real field and let Xtrans be X minus the union of all connected infinite semialgebraic subsets of X. For every ε > 0 there is C such that Xtrans has at most C·Hε rational points of height ≤ H.
André–Oort conjecture : a subvariety of a Shimura variety containing a Zariski-dense set of special points is itself special. Proved for ℂn by Pila (2011) and in general by Pila–Shankar–Tsimerman (2021, with an appendix by Esnault–Groechenig), using height bounds of Binyamini–Schmidt–Yafaev; all along the Pila–Zannier strategy built on o-minimal counting. 威尔基定理 （威尔基，1996）：ℝexp = (ℝ, +, ·, exp) 是模型完全且 o-极小的。（更早的有：半代数集，塔斯基 1930 年代；ℝan，van den Dries 1986，借助 Gabrielov 的结果；之后有 ℝan,exp，van den Dries–Macintyre–Marker 1994。）
皮拉–威尔基计数定理 （2006）。设 X ⊆ ℝn 可在实域的某个 o-极小扩张中定义，Xtrans 为 X 去掉其所有连通的无穷半代数子集之后的部分。则对每个 ε > 0 存在 C，使 Xtrans 中高度 ≤ H 的有理点至多 C·Hε 个。

Why people care / Motivation. Logic as a tool for geometry and number theory. o-minimality was Grothendieck's dream of a “tame topology” come true, and the counting theorem turned it into a machine for unlikely-intersection problems: André–Oort, Manin–Mumford re-proved, and parts of the Zilber–Pink conjecture , which is still open in general. Open questions also remain inside the theory, for example which further functions (e.g. Γ, ζ on suitable domains) generate o-minimal structures, and effective versions of Pila–Wilkie . 动机 / 为什么关心。逻辑成了几何与数论的工具。o-极小性实现了格罗滕迪克“驯顺拓扑”的梦想，而计数定理又把它变成了处理“不太可能的交”的利器：安德烈–奥尔特、马宁–芒福德的新证明，以及至今一般情形仍未解决的齐尔伯–平克猜想 的部分情形。理论内部也还有问题，例如哪些新的函数（如适当区域上的 Γ、ζ）能生成 o-极小结构，以及皮拉–威尔基定理的有效版本 。
⚠ Common pitfall. o-minimality is about definable sets, not about the functions you start with. sin restricted to [0, 1] is harmless (it is in ℝan), but sin on all of ℝ defines πℤ, then ℤ, and then all of arithmetic: the structure becomes as wild as (ℤ, +, ·). And the counting theorem says nothing about the algebraic part: the parabola has about H rational points, far more than Hε. ⚠ 常见误区。o-极小性说的是可定义集，不是最初给的那几个函数。限制在 [0, 1] 上的 sin 是无害的（它在 ℝan 中），但定义在整个 ℝ 上的 sin 能定义出 πℤ，进而定义 ℤ，进而定义全部算术：结构变得和 (ℤ, +, ·) 一样狂野。另外，计数定理对代数部分不做任何断言：抛物线上大约有 H 个有理点，远多于 Hε。

Exponentiation: Tarski's problem, Schanuel's conjecture, Zilber's program指数函数：塔斯基问题、沙努埃尔猜想、齐尔伯纲领

Prerequisites前置: §3 L11 · decidability可判定性 L12 · incompleteness不完全性
One idea. Tarski showed that the theory of (ℝ, +, ·) is decidable (quantifier elimination), and asked: what about (ℝ, +, ·, exp)? The answer hinges on a conjecture from transcendental number theory. Toggle the assumptions and watch which conclusions light up. 核心思想。塔斯基证明了 (ℝ, +, ·) 的理论可判定（量词消去），并问：(ℝ, +, ·, exp) 呢？答案竟取决于超越数论中的一个猜想。切换下面的假设，看看哪些结论会亮起来。
Schanuel's conjecture (SC) : if z₁, …, zn ∈ ℂ are linearly independent over ℚ, then the field ℚ(z₁, …, zn, ez₁, …, ezn) has transcendence degree ≥ n over ℚ. (Lindemann–Weierstrass, for algebraic zi, is a proved special case.) SC would imply that e and π are algebraically independent; today we do not even know whether e + π is irrational .
Tarski's exponential function problem : is Th(ℝexp) decidable? Macintyre–Wilkie 1996 : yes, if the real version of SC holds.
Zilber's quasiminimality conjecture : every subset of ℂ definable in ℂexp = (ℂ, +, ·, exp) is countable or co-countable. Zilber (2005) built a quasiminimal “pseudo-exponential” field Bexp (categorical in every uncountable cardinal, in Lω₁,ω(Q)), and conjectured ℂexp ≅ Bexp; this is equivalent to SC + “strong exponential-algebraic closedness”. Bays–Kirby 2018 : exponential-algebraic closedness of ℂexp (no SC needed) implies quasiminimality. 沙努埃尔猜想 (SC) ：若 z₁, …, zn ∈ ℂ 在 ℚ 上线性无关，则域 ℚ(z₁, …, zn, ez₁, …, ezn) 在 ℚ 上的超越次数 ≥ n。（zi 为代数数时就是林德曼–魏尔斯特拉斯定理，是已证明的特例。）SC 蕴涵 e 与 π 代数无关；而今天我们连 e + π 是否为无理数都不知道 。
塔斯基指数函数问题 ：Th(ℝexp) 可判定吗？Macintyre–Wilkie 1996 ：若实数版本的 SC 成立，则可判定。
齐尔伯拟极小性猜想 ：ℂexp = (ℂ, +, ·, exp) 中每个可定义的 ℂ 的子集都是可数或余可数的。齐尔伯（2005）构造了一个拟极小的“伪指数”域 Bexp（在 Lω₁,ω(Q) 中于每个不可数基数上范畴），并猜想 ℂexp ≅ Bexp；这等价于 SC 加上“强指数代数闭性”。Bays–Kirby 2018 ：ℂexp 的指数代数闭性（不需要 SC）蕴涵拟极小性。

Why people care / Motivation. The exponential function is where analysis, algebra and number theory collide, and model theory tries to show that ℂexp is “as tame as it could possibly be”. Zilber's trichotomy predicted that every non-trivial geometry comes from a group or a field; Hrushovski showed it fails in general, but it holds in Zariski geometries (Hrushovski–Zilber 1996), and that version powered Hrushovski's Mordell–Lang proof. The “restricted trichotomy” for structures interpreted in algebraically closed fields is now a in characteristic 0 (Castle, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 2024). For all characteristics there is a proof by Castle–Hasson–Ye (arXiv preprint 2024, not yet published as of 2026) . 动机 / 为什么关心。指数函数是分析、代数与数论交汇的地方，模型论试图证明 ℂexp“驯顺到了极致”。齐尔伯三分法预言：每个非平凡的几何都来自群或域；赫鲁晓夫斯基证明它一般不成立，但在 Zariski 几何中成立（Hrushovski–Zilber 1996），而正是这个版本支撑了赫鲁晓夫斯基对莫德尔–朗猜想的证明。对可在代数闭域中解释的结构，“受限三分法”在特征 0 时已是 （Castle，J. ⚠ Common pitfall. ℂexp is not tame in the first-order sense: it defines ℤ as {x : ∀y (ey = 1 → exy = 1)}, hence all of arithmetic, so its first-order theory is undecidable and unstable. Quasiminimality is a tameness statement about definable subsets of ℂ in a different sense (countable/co-countable), consistent with that wildness. Conversely ℝexp is o-minimal, yet its decidability is open. ⚠ 常见误区。ℂexp 在一阶意义下并不驯顺：它能定义 ℤ，即 {x : ∀y (ey = 1 → exy = 1)}，进而定义全部算术，所以它的一阶理论不可判定，也不稳定。拟极小性说的是 ℂ 的可定义子集在另一种意义下驯顺（可数或余可数），与上述狂野并不矛盾。反过来，ℝexp 是 o-极小的，但它的可判定性仍未解决。

NIP = finite VC dimension: shattering, learning, regularityNIP = 有限 VC 维：打散、学习、正则性

Prerequisites前置: §1 · NIP L5 · compactness紧致性
One idea. A family of sets shatters a finite set A if it can cut out every subset of A. The independence property says exactly that the family {φ(M, b)} shatters arbitrarily large finite sets. So NIP = “every definable family has finite VC dimension”, a notion invented independently in statistical learning theory. 核心思想。若一族集合能从有限集 A 中切出 A 的每一个子集，就说它打散了 A。独立性质恰好是说：集族 {φ(M, b)} 能打散任意大的有限集。所以 NIP =“每个可定义集族的 VC 维都有限”，而 VC 维正是统计学习理论中独立发明的概念。

Definition. A family 𝓕 of subsets of X shatters A ⊆ X if {F ∩ A : F ∈ 𝓕} = 𝒫(A). The VC dimension of 𝓕 is the largest size of a shattered finite set (∞ if unbounded).
Theorem (Laskowski 1992, from Shelah's work): T is NIP ⇔ for every φ(x; y) the family {φ(M, b) : b} has finite VC dimension.
Sauer–Shelah lemma (Sauer 1972; Shelah 1972; Vapnik–Chervonenkis 1971): if VC dimension is d, then on any n-element set 𝓕 cuts out at most Σi≤d C(n, i) = O(nd) subsets, instead of 2n. 定义. 设 𝓕 是 X 的子集族。若 {F ∩ A : F ∈ 𝓕} = 𝒫(A)，则称 𝓕 打散 A ⊆ X。𝓕 的 VC 维是能被打散的有限集的最大元素个数（无上界则为 ∞）。
定理 （Laskowski 1992，基于谢拉赫的工作）：T 是 NIP ⇔ 对每个 φ(x; y)，集族 {φ(M, b) : b} 的 VC 维有限。
Sauer–Shelah 引理 （Sauer 1972；Shelah 1972；Vapnik–Chervonenkis 1971）：若 VC 维为 d，则在任意 n 元集上 𝓕 至多切出 Σi≤d C(n, i) = O(nd) 个子集，而不是 2n 个。
Why people care / Motivation. The same dividing lines appear in three fields. Learning: a class is PAC-learnable ⇔ it has finite VC dimension (Blumer–Ehrenfeucht–Haussler–Warmuth 1989); finite Littlestone dimension, which corresponds to model-theoretic stability (Chase–Freitag 2019), characterizes online learnability and also differentially private learnability (Alon–Bun–Livni–Malliaris–Moran 2019–22). Combinatorics: stable graphs have regularity partitions with no irregular pairs (Malliaris–Shelah 2014), and bounded-VC graphs have near-homogeneous partitions (Alon–Fischer–Newman 2007, Lovász–Szegedy 2010). Ongoing program : transfer between “tame theories” and “tame combinatorics”, e.g. Erdős–Hajnal-type results for definable graphs in distal or NIP structures (Erdős–Hajnal in general is ). 动机 / 为什么关心。同样的分界线出现在三个领域。学习：一个概念类可 PAC 学习 ⇔ 它的 VC 维有限（Blumer–Ehrenfeucht–Haussler–Warmuth 1989）；有限的 Littlestone 维对应模型论中的稳定性（Chase–Freitag 2019），它刻画在线可学习性，也刻画差分隐私可学习性（Alon–Bun–Livni–Malliaris–Moran 2019–22）。组合：稳定图存在没有非正则对的正则划分（Malliaris–Shelah 2014），有界 VC 维的图存在近乎齐次的划分（Alon–Fischer–Newman 2007，Lovász–Szegedy 2010）。进行中的纲领 ：在“驯顺理论”与“驯顺组合”之间来回迁移，例如 distal 或 NIP 结构中可定义图的 Erdős–Hajnal 型结果（一般情形的 Erdős–Hajnal 猜想仍是 ）。
⚠ Common pitfall. To show VC dimension ≥ d, you need one d-point set that is shattered. To show VC dimension < d, you must show no d-point set is shattered. Finding one bad 3-point configuration for half-planes (three collinear points) proves nothing; three points in general position are shattered, so half-planes have VC dimension 3, not 2. ⚠ 常见误区。要证明 VC 维 ≥ d，只需找到一个被打散的 d 点集。要证明 VC 维 < d，必须证明没有任何 d 点集被打散。对半平面族找到一个打不散的 3 点构型（三点共线）什么也证明不了；处于一般位置的三点是能被打散的，所以半平面族的 VC 维是 3，不是 2。

One idea. “Does this polynomial equation have a solution in R?” is always semi-decidable for a countable computable ring R: search, and stop if you find one. The question is whether you can also always say no. MRDP theorem (Davis, Putnam, Robinson 1961; Matiyasevich 1970): a set S ⊆ ℕ is computably enumerable ⇔ it is Diophantine, i.e. S = {a : ∃x̄ ∈ ℕm p(a, x̄) = 0} for some integer polynomial p. Hence there is no algorithm deciding solvability of Diophantine equations over ℤ.
Rings of integers : for every number field K, H10 over 𝒪K is undecidable. This was proved by Koymans–Pagano (arXiv Dec 2024; J. Amer. Math. Soc., online Aug 2026), who in fact cover every infinite ring finitely generated over ℤ, and independently by Alpöge–Bhargava–Ho–Shnidman (arXiv Jan 2025; Invent. Math. 2026) by a different method. Earlier: many special cases (Denef, Lipshitz, Pheidas, Shlapentokh, Videla; Mazur–Rubin 2010 conditional on Shafarevich–Tate).
H10 over ℚ as of 2025. An AI-generated manuscript in the OpenAI release (dated 24 Sept 2026, revised 6 Oct 2026) claims undecidability . It has no Lean formalization and has not been refereed or independently verified, although a follow-up preprint (Z.-W. Sun, Oct 2026) already builds on it. Known: it would follow if ℤ were Diophantine (existentially definable) in ℚ; Mazur's conjecture on real topology of rational points implies ℤ is not Diophantine in ℚ, so a proof must avoid that route. MRDP 定理 （Davis、Putnam、Robinson 1961；马季亚谢维奇 1970）：集合 S ⊆ ℕ 可计算枚举 ⇔ 它是丢番图集，即存在整系数多项式 p 使 S = {a : ∃x̄ ∈ ℕm p(a, x̄) = 0}。因此不存在判定 ℤ 上丢番图方程可解性的算法。
整数环 ：对每个数域 K，𝒪K 上的第十问题都不可判定。这由 Koymans–Pagano 证明（2024 年 12 月 arXiv；J. Amer. Math. Soc.，2026 年 8 月在线发表），他们实际上覆盖了所有在 ℤ 上有限生成的无穷环；Alpöge–Bhargava–Ho–Shnidman 用不同方法独立证明（2025 年 1 月 arXiv；Invent. Math. 2026）。此前已有许多特例（Denef、Lipshitz、Pheidas、Shlapentokh、Videla；Mazur–Rubin 2010 在沙法列维奇–泰特猜想的假设下）。

Why people care / Motivation. Hilbert asked for an algorithm in 1900; MRDP showed that number theory contains the halting problem, so “every c.e. set is a polynomial's solution set”. Over ℚ the question is about rational points, the central object of arithmetic geometry: undecidability there would say no algorithm can determine whether an arbitrary variety has a rational point. Over ℚ there is also a geometric reason to expect a hard fight (Mazur, Poonen). 动机 / 为什么关心。希尔伯特在 1900 年要求一个算法；MRDP 定理表明数论里藏着停机问题，即“每个可计算可枚举集都是某个多项式的解集”。在 ℚ 上，问题关乎有理点，这是算术几何的核心对象：若不可判定，就意味着没有算法能判断任意代数簇是否有有理点。在 ℚ 上也有几何上的理由让人预期这是一场硬仗（马祖尔、Poonen）。
⚠ Common pitfall. Undecidability is about a uniform algorithm for all equations. Each single equation either has a solution or not, and many specific equations are solved (x² + y² = 3 has no rational solutions). Also, solvability over ℝ or ℂ is decidable (Tarski), and so is the full theory of ℚp (Ax–Kochen, Ershov 1965), so “undecidable” is not a property of polynomials as such. ⚠ 常见误区。不可判定说的是对所有方程统一适用的算法不存在。每个具体方程要么有解要么无解，而且许多具体方程已经解决了（x² + y² = 3 没有有理解）。另外，在 ℝ 或 ℂ 上的可解性是可判定的（塔斯基），ℚp 的整个理论也可判定（Ax–Kochen、Ershov 1965），所以“不可判定”并不是多项式本身的属性。

Martin's conjecture (ZF + AD + DC). The Big Five (Friedman, Simpson and many others, 1970s–): RCA₀ (Δ⁰₁-comprehension + Σ⁰₁-induction) < WKL₀ (+ weak Kőnig's lemma) < ACA₀ (arithmetical comprehension) < ATR₀ (arithmetical transfinite recursion) < Π¹₁-CA₀. Proof-theoretic ordinals: ωω, ωω, ε₀, Γ₀, ψ(Ωω).

Why people care / Motivation. Ramsey-type theorems are the main source of exceptions to the Big Five pattern, and studying them has produced new forcing techniques in computability. 动机 / 为什么关心。反推数学衡量普通定理的逻辑成本，并告诉我们有多少数学能在弱的、有哲学动机的系统中存活：WKL₀ 在 PRA 上是 Π⁰₂-保守的，所以其中可证的定理是“可有穷归约的”（Simpson 所说的希尔伯特纲领的部分实现）。拉姆齐型定理是五大格局的主要例外来源，研究它们催生了可计算性中新的力迫技术。

One idea. A finite string is random if it has no description much shorter than itself. An infinite sequence is random if none of its prefixes can be compressed by more than a constant. Definitions. K(σ) is the prefix-free Kolmogorov complexity of σ (length of a shortest program for σ on a fixed universal prefix-free machine). X ∈ 2ω is Martin-Löf random if it avoids every effectively null Gδ set given by a uniformly Σ⁰₁ test; equivalently (Levin–Schnorr) ∃c ∀n K(X↾n) ≥ n − c. Why people care / Motivation. It gives a mathematically exact meaning to “this individual sequence is random”, which probability theory alone cannot do. One idea. Gentzen (1936) measured PA by an ordinal: PA cannot prove the well-foundedness of ε₀ (for its notation system), and a weak base theory plus induction up to ε₀ proves Con(PA). Why people care / Motivation. An ordinal analysis is a consistency proof with a precise price tag (“Con(T) follows from the well-foundedness of this explicit ordering”), the closest thing we have to Hilbert's program after Gödel. It also yields concrete information: the provably total functions of T (for PA: those dominated by some fα, α < ε₀, from L21) and independence results (§12). The Π¹₂ wall is where the needed ordinal notations must reflect very strong set-theoretic “stability” properties. 动机 / 为什么关心。序数分析是附带精确价签的一致性证明（“Con(T) 可由这个明确序关系的良基性推出”），是哥德尔之后我们离希尔伯特纲领最近的东西。它还给出具体信息：T 的可证全函数（对 PA 而言，就是被第 21 课中某个 fα（α < ε₀）控制的函数），以及独立性结果（§12）。Π¹₂ 之墙所在之处，所需的序数记号必须反映极强的集合论“稳定性”性质。
⚠ Common pitfall. “PA cannot prove ε₀ is well-ordered” is about a specific notation system and a specific formalization; ε₀ as a set-theoretic ordinal is of course well-ordered in ZFC. And an ordinal analysis does not make a theory “more certain” by magic: it reduces trust in T to trust in one transparent principle (transfinite induction along one ordering) over a weak base. ⚠ 常见误区。“PA 证明不了 ε₀ 是良序”针对的是一个具体的记号系统和具体的形式化；作为集合论中的序数，ε₀ 在 ZFC 中当然是良序的。序数分析也不会魔术般地让理论“更可靠”：它把对 T 的信任，归约为在弱基础理论上对一条透明原则（沿某个序关系的超限归纳）的信任。

One idea. Gödel's sentence is true but artificial. The program of concrete incompleteness looks for statements that mathematicians would care about anyway (about numbers, trees, graphs) and that are nonetheless unprovable in a standard theory. The proofs of unprovability go through the ordinals of §11. 核心思想。哥德尔句真而做作。具体不完全性纲领寻找这样的命题：数学家本来就会关心（关于数、树、图），却在某个标准理论中不可证。不可证性的证明要经过 §11 中的序数。

The ladder of concrete independence具体独立性的阶梯

Goodstein's theorem (Goodstein 1944): every Goodstein sequence reaches 0. It is not provable in PA (Kirby–Paris 1982).
Paris–Harrington (1977): the “large homogeneous set” version of the finite Ramsey theorem is true but not provable in PA.
Friedman : finite forms of Kruskal's tree theorem are not provable in ATR₀ (1980s); the graph minor theorem (Robertson–Seymour) is not provable in Π¹₁-CA₀ (Friedman–Robertson–Seymour 1987).
Boolean relation theory (Friedman, 1990s–): explicit statements about functions on ℕ provable only with large cardinal hypotheses (Mahlo cardinals of all finite orders), proposed as “concrete mathematics needing large cardinals”. 古德斯坦定理 （古德斯坦 1944）：每个古德斯坦序列都会到达 0。 它在 PA 中不可证（Kirby–Paris 1982）。
帕里斯–哈灵顿定理 （1977）：有限拉姆齐定理的“大齐次集”版本为真，但在 PA 中不可证。
弗里德曼 ：克鲁斯卡尔树定理的有限形式在 ATR₀ 中不可证（1980 年代）；图子式定理（Robertson–Seymour）在 Π¹₁-CA₀ 中不可证（Friedman–Robertson–Seymour 1987）。
布尔关系理论 （弗里德曼，1990 年代起）：关于 ℕ 上函数的明确命题，只有借助大基数假设（所有有限阶的马洛基数）才能证明，被提出作为“需要大基数的具体数学”。
Why people care / Motivation. These results answer the working mathematician's objection “incompleteness never affects real mathematics”. They show that natural combinatorial principles can encode consistency strength, and they connect logic to the question of which axioms (PA? ZFC? large cardinals?) mathematics actually needs. The open frontier : find a widely accepted, natural statement of finite combinatorics that needs large cardinals, or one independent of ZFC itself. 动机 / 为什么关心。这些结果回应了一线数学家的质疑：“不完全性从不影响真正的数学”。它们表明自然的组合原理可以编码一致性强度，并把逻辑与“数学究竟需要哪些公理（PA？ZFC？大基数？）”这个问题联系起来。开放的前沿 ：找到一个被广泛接受的、自然的有限组合命题，它需要大基数，或独立于 ZFC 本身。
⚠ Common pitfall. “Independent of PA” does not mean “of unknown truth”. Goodstein's theorem and Paris–Harrington are true (provable in ZFC, even in fairly weak second-order systems); they are just not provable from PA's axioms. Contrast this with CH (L16), whose truth value is not settled by ZFC at all. ⚠ 常见误区。“独立于 PA”不等于“真假未知”。古德斯坦定理与帕里斯–哈灵顿定理都是真的（在 ZFC 中可证，甚至在相当弱的二阶系统中可证）；只是从 PA 的公理出发证不出来。与之对照的是 CH（第 16 课），ZFC 根本不决定它的真假。

Speculation zone (clearly labelled). Opinions differ on whether formal verification will become standard for research papers, whether AI systems will routinely prove research-level theorems, and whether HoTT/univalent foundations will replace set theory as the everyday foundation. None of these is a mathematical statement with a status; they are predictions. What is not speculation: a checked formal proof reduces trust to (i) the small kernel, (ii) the axioms used, and (iii) the faithfulness of the formal statement. 推测区（明确标注）。形式化验证会不会成为研究论文的标配？AI 系统会不会常规地证明研究级定理？HoTT/泛等基础会不会取代集合论成为日常基础？对这些问题看法不一。它们都不是带“状态”的数学命题，而是预测。不属于推测的是：一个通过检查的形式证明，把信任归结为 (i) 小小的内核，(ii) 所用的公理，(iii) 形式陈述是否忠实于原意。
Why people care / Motivation. Proofs are getting longer and more collaborative, and refereeing cannot keep up; formal checking scales. Foundations matter again: Lean's Mathlib is built on dependent type theory, not ZFC, and how the two relate (they are mutually interpretable up to universe/large-cardinal assumptions) is now a practical question. One idea. Most open problems in logic come in four shapes: classification (how many / what kinds of objects?), independence (is it decided by our axioms?), decidability (is there an algorithm?), and structure (what does this order or hierarchy look like?). Filter by area, by type, or by status. 核心思想。逻辑中的大多数未解决问题有四种形状：分类（有多少、有哪几种对象？）、独立性（我们的公理能否判定它？）、可判定性（有没有算法？）和结构（这个序或层级长什么样？）。可以按领域、类型或状态筛选。
⚠ Common pitfall. “Open” problems are not all alike. Before working on a problem, find out which kind it is. ⚠ 常见误区。“未解决”的问题并不都一样。有些在精确意义上被预期独立于 ZFC（CH 已经如此），有些是算术命题，因而有确定的真值（P ≠ NP 是一个关于程序与运行时间的 Π⁰₂ 命题），还有些是没有是非形式的研究纲领。动手研究一个问题之前，先弄清它属于哪一种。
