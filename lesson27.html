§1

A map of the frontier前沿地图

Prerequisites前置: L16 · forcing, Con(¬CH)力迫与 Con(¬CH) L25 · large cardinals & determinacy大基数与决定性
One idea. After Gödel and Cohen, the field split into a few big questions. Can new axioms settle what ZFC leaves open (CH first)? How far do canonical models of large cardinals reach? What does arithmetic of infinite cardinals look like when ZFC leaves room? The tiles below are the landmarks. Click one to jump to its step, or filter by status. 核心思想。哥德尔与科恩之后，这个领域分化出几个大问题：新公理能否判定 ZFC 判定不了的命题（首先是 CH）？大基数的典范模型能造到多高？在 ZFC 留下余地的地方，无穷基数的算术长什么样？下面每块砖是一个地标。点击就跳到对应的步骤，也可以按状态筛选。
Why people care / 动机. ZFC is the accepted foundation of mathematics, yet it cannot answer “how many real numbers are there?” This is not a curiosity. Questions from analysis, algebra and topology hit the same wall (§11). The frontier problems ask: what should we add to ZFC, if anything, and how do we tell a good new axiom from an arbitrary one? 动机。ZFC 是公认的数学基础，却回答不了“实数有多少个”。这不是什么猎奇问题：分析、代数、拓扑中的问题也会撞上同一堵墙（见 §11）。前沿问题追问的是：要不要给 ZFC 添加新公理？添加什么？又如何区分好的新公理和任意拍脑袋的公理？
⚠ Common pitfall. “Independent of ZFC” is not the same as “open”. CH's independence is a theorem (Gödel 1938, Cohen 1963). What is open is whether some justified extension of ZFC settles it, and that question has a mathematical part and a philosophical part. ⚠ 常见误区。“独立于 ZFC”不等于“未解决”。CH 的独立性是一条定理（哥德尔 1938，科恩 1963）。真正悬而未决的是：有没有某个站得住脚的 ZFC 扩张能判定 CH？这个问题既有数学的一面，也有哲学的一面。
§2

The continuum problem after Cohen, and why large cardinals can't settle it科恩之后的连续统问题，以及大基数为何判定不了它

Prerequisites前置: L15 · L ⊨ GCHL ⊨ GCH L16 · forcing, Cohen reals力迫与科恩实数 L24 · measurable cardinals可测基数
One idea. Gödel hoped that large cardinal axioms would decide CH. They cannot, for a simple reason: CH lives at the bottom of the universe (sets of size about 2^ℵ₀), large cardinals live far above, and a small forcing can change the bottom without touching the top. 核心思想。哥德尔曾希望大基数公理能判定 CH。这做不到，原因很简单：CH 住在宇宙的底层（规模约为 2^ℵ₀ 的集合），大基数住在极高处，而小力迫可以改动底层，却碰不到顶层。

Watch which part of the picture moves. Then try the last button, a large forcing, for contrast. 以任意顺序按下力迫按钮。左边的仪表显示 2^ℵ₀，右边的梯子显示当前模型里的大基数，灰色条表示力迫偏序的大小。看看图中哪一部分在动。最后按一下“大”力迫的按钮作对比。

Theorem (Lévy–Solovay 1967) THEOREM. If κ is measurable and ℙ is a forcing notion with |ℙ| < κ, then κ remains measurable in every extension V[G]. Analogous preservation results hold for the standard large cardinals (Woodin, supercompact, extendible, …).
Corollary INDEPENDENT. Let LC be any of these large cardinal axioms. If ZFC + LC is consistent, so are ZFC + LC + CH and ZFC + LC + ¬CH. Reason: Add(ω, ω₂) (forces ¬CH) and Col(ω₁, 2^ℵ₀) (forces CH) both have size below the least inaccessible above 2^ℵ₀, hence below κ. 定理（列维–索洛维 1967） 定理。若 κ 是可测基数，ℙ 是满足 |ℙ| < κ 的力迫偏序，则 κ 在每个扩张 V[G] 中仍是可测基数。对常见的大基数（伍丁、超紧、可扩……）也有类似的保持定理。
推论 独立。设 LC 是上述任一大基数公理。若 ZFC + LC 一致，则 ZFC + LC + CH 与 ZFC + LC + ¬CH 都一致。理由：Add(ω, ω₂)（迫使 ¬CH）与 Col(ω₁, 2^ℵ₀)（迫使 CH）的大小都小于 2^ℵ₀ 之上的第一个不可达基数，因而小于 κ。
Why people care / 动机. Gödel's program (1947): find new axioms, justified by their consequences and intrinsic plausibility, that decide what ZFC leaves open. Large cardinals were the first candidates, and they did spectacularly well on definable sets of reals (§3). Lévy–Solovay shows they will never reach CH on their own. Any solution needs a different kind of axiom. §4 and §5 are the two main candidates. 动机。哥德尔纲领（1947）：寻找新公理，用它们的推论和内在合理性来论证其正当性，用它们去判定 ZFC 判定不了的命题。大基数是第一批候选者，在可定义实数集上战果辉煌（见 §3）。列维–索洛维定理表明，单靠大基数永远够不到 CH。任何解决方案都需要另一类公理。§4 和 §5 是两个主要候选。
⚠ Common pitfall. “Large cardinals don't affect CH” does not mean they don't affect the reals. A measurable already implies every Σ¹₂ set of reals is Lebesgue measurable and has the perfect set property, so there is no Σ¹₂ counterexample to CH. What large cardinals can't touch is CH for arbitrary sets of reals. ⚠ 常见误区。“大基数不影响 CH”并不意味着它们不影响实数。一个可测基数就已推出每个 Σ¹₂ 实数集都勒贝格可测且有完美集性质，所以不存在 Σ¹₂ 的 CH 反例。大基数够不着的，是关于任意实数集的 CH。
§3

Generic absoluteness: what large cardinals do freeze力迫绝对性：大基数能冻结什么

Prerequisites前置: L19 · projective hierarchy投影层级 L20 · absoluteness, Shoenfield绝对性、Shoenfield L25 · Woodin cardinals, AD^L(ℝ)伍丁基数、AD^L(ℝ)
One idea. Forcing is our main tool for proving independence. If a statement has the same truth value in every forcing extension, forcing can never show it independent. Large cardinals “freeze” more and more levels of complexity in this way, up to all of L(ℝ). CH sits exactly one level above, at Σ²₁. 核心思想。力迫是证明独立性的主要工具。如果一个命题在每个力迫扩张中真值都不变，力迫就永远无法证明它独立。大基数以这种方式“冻结”越来越高的复杂度层次，一直到整个 L(ℝ)。而 CH 恰好位于再高一层：Σ²₁。

Drag the slider to add large cardinal strength. A level turns blue with a lock when its truth values can no longer be changed by set forcing. Toggle “assume CH” at the top setting to see Woodin's conditional result. 拖动滑块来增加大基数强度。某一层变成蓝色并上锁，表示它的真值再也不能被集合力迫改变。在最高档位打开“假设 CH”，看伍丁的条件性结果。

Theorem (Woodin) THEOREM. Assume there is a proper class of Woodin cardinals. Then for every sentence φ and every set forcing ℙ: L(ℝ) ⊨ φ iff L(ℝ)V[G] ⊨ φ. In particular every projective statement has the same truth value in all set-forcing extensions, and AD holds in L(ℝ).
Theorem (Woodin 1985, Σ²₁ absoluteness) THEOREM. Assume there is a proper class of measurable Woodin cardinals. If a Σ²₁ sentence holds in some set-forcing extension satisfying CH, it holds in every set-forcing extension satisfying CH. (In Ω-logic language: ZFC + CH is Ω-complete for Σ²₁.)
Σ²₁ sentences: “there is a set of reals A such that (projective statement about A)”. CH itself is Σ²₁: “there is a well-ordering of ℝ all of whose proper initial segments are countable”. 定理（伍丁） 定理。设存在真类多个伍丁基数。则对任意语句 φ 和任意集合力迫 ℙ：L(ℝ) ⊨ φ 当且仅当 L(ℝ)V[G] ⊨ φ。特别地，每个投影命题在所有集合力迫扩张中真值相同，并且 L(ℝ) 满足 AD。
定理（伍丁 1985，Σ²₁ 绝对性） 定理。设存在真类多个可测伍丁基数。若某个 Σ²₁ 语句在某个满足 CH 的集合力迫扩张中成立，则它在每个满足 CH 的集合力迫扩张中都成立。（用 Ω-逻辑的话说：ZFC + CH 对 Σ²₁ 是 Ω-完全的。）
Σ²₁ 语句形如“存在实数集 A，使得（关于 A 的某个投影命题）”。CH 本身就是 Σ²₁ 的：“存在 ℝ 的一个良序，其每个真前段都可数”。
Why people care / 动机. This is the strongest evidence that large cardinals are “the right axioms” for second-order arithmetic: they make the theory of the projective sets as complete as forcing can test, and the answers agree with the determinacy picture of L25. Woodin's Σ²₁ theorem says CH is the natural next question: once you accept CH, the entire Σ²₁ level becomes frozen as well. That is one argument for CH. The opposing camp asks why CH, and not some Π²₁ axiom, should get this privileged role. 动机。这是“大基数是二阶算术的正确公理”最有力的证据：它们让投影集合的理论在力迫所能检验的范围内完全确定下来，而且给出的答案与第 25 课决定性的图景一致。伍丁的 Σ²₁ 定理说明 CH 是自然的下一个问题：一旦接受 CH，整个 Σ²₁ 层也被冻结。这是支持 CH 的一条论证；反方则会问：凭什么是 CH，而不是某条 Π²₁ 公理，享有这种特殊地位？
⚠ Common pitfall. “Frozen under forcing” is not “provable”. Gödel's incompleteness still applies: Con(ZFC + LC) is an arithmetic sentence that ZFC + LC does not prove (if consistent), even though forcing can't change it. Generic absoluteness kills forcing-style independence only. ⚠ 常见误区。“在力迫下冻结”不等于“可证明”。哥德尔不完全性定理依然有效：Con(ZFC + LC) 是算术语句，ZFC + LC（若一致）证明不了它，尽管力迫改变不了它的真值。力迫绝对性消灭的只是力迫式的独立性。
§4

Candidate answer I: forcing axioms give 2^ℵ₀ = ℵ₂候选答案一：力迫公理给出 2^ℵ₀ = ℵ₂

Prerequisites前置: L16 · dense sets, generic filters稠密集与脱殊滤 L9 · ℵ₁, ℵ₂, cofinalityℵ₁、ℵ₂ 与共尾性
One idea. A forcing axiom says: for a class of “nice” forcings, generic filters (meeting ℵ₁ dense sets) already exist in V. It is a Baire category theorem one cardinal higher: “the universe is saturated; whatever could be forced into existence, already exists”. The bigger the class of forcings, the stronger the axiom, and the strong ones pin 2^ℵ₀ at exactly ℵ₂. 核心思想。力迫公理说：对某一类“好”的力迫，（与 ℵ₁ 个稠密集都相交的）脱殊滤在 V 中已经存在。这是往上提了一个基数的贝尔纲定理：“宇宙是饱和的，凡是能力迫出来的东西，早已存在。”力迫的类越大，公理越强，而强的力迫公理把 2^ℵ₀ 恰好钉在 ℵ₂。

Click a class of forcings. Each class contains the ones inside it. 点击一类力迫。每一类都包含它内部的各类。

Definition. For a class Γ of forcings, FA(Γ): for every ℙ ∈ Γ and every family 𝒟 of at most ℵ₁ dense subsets of ℙ, there is a filter on ℙ meeting every member of 𝒟. MAℵ₁ = FA(ccc), PFA = FA(proper), MM = FA(stationary-set-preserving). MM⁺⁺ strengthens MM by also asking the filter to interpret ℵ₁ names for stationary subsets of ω₁ as stationary sets.
Theorems THEOREM. MAℵ₁ is consistent relative to ZFC alone (Martin–Solovay 1970; Solovay–Tennenbaum 1971) and does not decide 2^ℵ₀ beyond 2^ℵ₀ ≥ ℵ₂. PFA and MM are consistent relative to a supercompact cardinal (Baumgartner; Foreman–Magidor–Shelah 1988). MM ⇒ 2^ℵ₀ = ℵ₂ (Foreman–Magidor–Shelah 1988); PFA ⇒ 2^ℵ₀ = ℵ₂ (Todorčević; Veličković); even the bounded version BPFA implies it (Moore 2005). PFA ⇒ SCH (Viale 2006). 定义. 对一类力迫 Γ，FA(Γ) 指：对每个 ℙ ∈ Γ 以及 ℙ 的任何一族至多 ℵ₁ 个稠密子集 𝒟，存在 ℙ 上的滤与 𝒟 的每个成员相交。MAℵ₁ = FA(ccc)，PFA = FA(真力迫)（真力迫公理），MM = FA(保平稳集)（马丁极大）。MM⁺⁺ 加强 MM：还要求这个滤把 ℵ₁ 个“ω₁ 的平稳子集”的名字解释成真正的平稳集。
定理 定理。MAℵ₁ 相对于 ZFC 本身一致（Martin–Solovay 1970；Solovay–Tennenbaum 1971），它对 2^ℵ₀ 除了 2^ℵ₀ ≥ ℵ₂ 之外什么也不决定。PFA 与 MM 相对于一个超紧基数一致（Baumgartner；Foreman–Magidor–Shelah 1988）。MM ⇒ 2^ℵ₀ = ℵ₂（Foreman–Magidor–Shelah 1988）；PFA ⇒ 2^ℵ₀ = ℵ₂（Todorčević；Veličković）；连有界版本 BPFA 也能推出它（Moore 2005）。PFA ⇒ SCH（Viale 2006）。
Woodin's axiom (*) (1999): AD holds in L(ℝ) and L(P(ω₁)) is a ℙmax-generic extension of L(ℝ). It implies 2^ℵ₀ = ℵ₂ and makes the theory of H(ω₂) “as rich as possible” in a precise sense (Π₂-maximality).
Theorem (Asperó–Schindler, Annals of Mathematics 2021) THEOREM. MM⁺⁺ ⇒ (*). Two rival routes to ℵ₂, one combinatorial (MM) and one descriptive-set-theoretic ((*)), turned out to point the same way.
Open OPEN. The exact consistency strength of PFA. Known: a supercompact suffices; PFA implies inner models far beyond many Woodin cardinals (core model induction, e.g. Jensen–Schimmerling–Schindler–Steel 2009); standard iterations need at least a strongly compact (Viale–Weiß 2011). Whether a supercompact is necessary is open. 伍丁的 (*) 公理（1999）：L(ℝ) 满足 AD，并且 L(P(ω₁)) 是 L(ℝ) 的 ℙmax-脱殊扩张。它推出 2^ℵ₀ = ℵ₂，并在精确的意义下（Π₂-极大性）使 H(ω₂) 的理论“尽可能丰富”。
定理（Asperó–Schindler，《数学年刊》2021） 定理。MM⁺⁺ ⇒ (*)。两条通往 ℵ₂ 的竞争路线，一条组合式（MM），一条描述集合论式（(*)），原来指向同一个方向。
未解决 未解决。PFA 的精确一致性强度。已知：一个超紧基数足够；PFA 推出远超多个伍丁基数的内模型（核心模型归纳，例如 Jensen–Schimmerling–Schindler–Steel 2009）；标准的迭代方法至少需要一个强紧基数（Viale–Weiß 2011）。超紧基数是否必需，尚不清楚。
Why people care / 动机. Maximality: a forcing axiom says that V is closed under every “reasonable” way of adding objects, so existence questions about structures of size ℵ₁ get decided uniformly. The axioms are also useful: they settle many problems in topology, algebra and operator algebras (§11), and the answers fit together. For many researchers, that coherence plus 2^ℵ₀ = ℵ₂ makes this the leading answer to the continuum problem. 动机。极大性：力迫公理说 V 对一切“合理的”添加对象的方式都封闭，于是关于 ℵ₁ 规模结构的存在性问题被统一地判定。这些公理也很好用：它们解决了拓扑、代数、算子代数中的大量问题（见 §11），而且答案相互吻合。在许多研究者看来，这种协调一致再加上 2^ℵ₀ = ℵ₂，使它成为连续统问题的头号候选答案。
⚠ Common pitfall. “MM is the strongest forcing axiom” has a precise meaning: for some forcing that fails to preserve stationary subsets of ω₁, the corresponding forcing axiom (even for ℵ₁ dense sets) is false in ZFC (Foreman–Magidor–Shelah). So the class can't be enlarged in this direction. Stronger variants exist (MM⁺⁺, axioms for larger cardinals), but they strengthen the conclusion, not the class. ⚠ 常见误区。“MM 是最强的力迫公理”有精确含义：存在某个不保持 ω₁ 平稳子集的力迫，其力迫公理（即使只要求 ℵ₁ 个稠密集）在 ZFC 中不成立（Foreman–Magidor–Shelah）。所以力迫的类不能在这个方向上再扩大。更强的变体（MM⁺⁺、关于更大基数的公理）确实存在，但它们加强的是结论，而不是力迫的类。
§5

Candidate answer II: Ultimate L gives CH. The fork候选答案二：终极 L 给出 CH。岔路口

Prerequisites前置: L15 · V = L decides CHV = L 判定 CH L22 · HODHOD §4
One idea. V = L decides CH (it implies GCH) but contradicts measurable cardinals. Woodin's program asks for an L-like axiom, “V = Ultimate L”, that is compatible with all large cardinals. If it exists and is accepted, CH is true. So the field faces a fork: maximize the universe (forcing axioms, ℵ₂) or make it canonical (Ultimate L, ℵ₁). 核心思想。V = L 判定了 CH（它推出 GCH），但与可测基数矛盾。伍丁的纲领要寻找一条像 L 一样的公理“V = 终极 L”，它要与一切大基数相容。如果它存在并被接受，CH 就为真。于是领域面临一个岔路口：把宇宙做到极大（力迫公理，ℵ₂），还是让宇宙典范（终极 L，ℵ₁）？

Theorem (Woodin) THEOREM. V = Ultimate L implies CH, and also V = HOD. (Proof idea: ¬CH is a Σ₂ sentence; if it held in V, it would hold in some HODL(A,ℝ), but CH holds in all such models under AD⁺.)
Ultimate-L Conjecture (Woodin) OPEN. (ZFC) If δ is an extendible cardinal, then (provably) there is a transitive class N such that N is a weak extender model for “δ is supercompact”, N ⊨ V = Ultimate L, and N ⊆ HOD.

Ω 猜想（伍丁） 未解决。在存在真类多个伍丁基数时，Ω-有效等同于 Ω-可证。伍丁 2001 年反对 CH 的论证假设了这个猜想（的强形式）。
Why people care / 动机. If the Ultimate-L Conjecture is proved, then ZFC + V = Ultimate L is compatible with every large cardinal we know, and (Woodin argues) it would settle essentially all questions that forcing can make independent. That would be a new “V = L” without L's defect. Woodin's 2025 Takagi Lectures (published 2026) present this as a realistic program. Critics reply that canonicity is not evidence of truth, and that forcing axioms have the richer mathematical consequences. 动机。如果终极 L 猜想得证，ZFC + V = 终极 L 就与我们知道的所有大基数相容，而且（伍丁认为）它将判定几乎所有能被力迫弄成独立的问题。那会是一个没有 L 的缺陷的新“V = L”。伍丁在 2025 年的高木讲座（2026 年发表）中把它当作一个现实可行的纲领。批评者则回应：典范性不等于真理的证据，而且力迫公理的数学推论更丰富。
⚠ Common pitfall. Nobody has proved CH is true, and nobody has proved 2^ℵ₀ = ℵ₂. The theorems are implications: MM ⇒ 2^ℵ₀ = ℵ₂, V = Ultimate L ⇒ CH. Choosing between the antecedents is the open part, and that choice is partly PHILOSOPHICAL. ⚠ 常见误区。没有人证明了 CH 为真，也没有人证明了 2^ℵ₀ = ℵ₂。定理只是蕴涵式：MM ⇒ 2^ℵ₀ = ℵ₂，V = 终极 L ⇒ CH。在两个前件之间作选择才是悬而未决的部分，而这个选择部分是哲学性的。

The inner model problem: how high can canonical models reach?内模型问题：典范模型能造到多高？

Prerequisites前置: L15 · L, condensationL 与凝聚性 L24 · L[U], ultrapowersL[U] 与超幂 L25 · consistency ladder一致性强度阶梯
One idea. For each large cardinal, build the smallest, most L-like universe containing it: an inner model with a fine structure, in which GCH and much more can be computed. Each rung reached this way is a triumph; the rung of a supercompact cardinal is still out of reach, and Ultimate L (§5) is the conjectured model at the very top. 核心思想。对每个大基数，构造包含它的最小、最像 L 的宇宙：一个具有精细结构的内模型，在其中 GCH 等许多性质都能算清楚。用这种方式每登上一级阶梯都是一次胜利；而超紧基数这一级至今仍够不着，终极 L（§5）就是猜想中位于最顶端的那个模型。

Drag the year slider. The frontier line marks where inner models with proved iterability stop; the dashed band marks constructions that work under extra iterability hypotheses. 拖动年份滑块。实线前沿表示已证明可迭代性的内模型到达的位置；虚线带表示在额外的可迭代性假设下可行的构造。

Landmarks THEOREM: L (Gödel 1938); L[U] for one measurable (Silver 1971, Kunen 1970); models for many measurables / Mitchell order (Mitchell 1974–84); a Woodin cardinal (Mitchell–Steel 1994); finitely and infinitely many Woodins (Steel and others, 1990s); iterability up to a Woodin limit of Woodin cardinals (Neeman 2002). Beyond that, fine structure is developed past superstrong and subcompact levels, but the needed iterability is assumed rather than proved.
Inner model problem for a supercompact OPEN. Construct a canonical (fine-structural, iterable) inner model with a supercompact cardinal. Extender models with only “short” extenders cannot do this; models with long extenders are under development (Woodin; Neeman–Steel; Schlutzenberg), and it is not known whether those constructions succeed. 里程碑 定理：L（哥德尔 1938）；一个可测基数的 L[U]（Silver 1971，Kunen 1970）；多个可测基数 / Mitchell 序的模型（Mitchell 1974–84）；一个伍丁基数（Mitchell–Steel 1994）；有限多个和无穷多个伍丁基数（Steel 等，1990 年代）；可迭代性一直证到伍丁基数之极限的伍丁基数（Neeman 2002）。再往上，精细结构理论已推进到超强基数和次紧基数之上，但所需的可迭代性是假设的，而非证明的。
超紧基数的内模型问题 未解决。构造一个具有超紧基数的典范（有精细结构、可迭代的）内模型。只用“短”扩张子的扩张子模型做不到这一点；使用长扩张子的模型正在发展中（伍丁；Neeman–Steel；Schlutzenberg），这些构造能否成功尚不可知。
Why people care / 动机. (1) Inner models give lower bounds: to show a statement needs a Woodin cardinal, build the core model and show the statement fails without one. That is how equiconsistencies like “¬SCH ≡ o(κ) = κ⁺⁺” (§9) are proved. (2) An inner model is a certificate that the large cardinal is coherent, an L-like picture of it. (3) Woodin's universality theorem suggests that a model reaching one supercompact would automatically reach all larger cardinals, which is why the supercompact rung is the decisive one. 动机。(1) 内模型提供下界：要证明某命题需要一个伍丁基数，就构造核心模型，再证明没有伍丁基数时该命题不成立。“¬SCH ≡ o(κ) = κ⁺⁺”（§9）这类等一致性正是这样证明的。(2) 内模型是大基数自洽的证书，是它的一幅像 L 一样的画像。(3) 伍丁的普遍性定理暗示：一个能达到超紧基数的模型会自动达到所有更大的基数，所以超紧这一级才是决定性的。
⚠ Common pitfall. An inner model for a large cardinal is not evidence that V is that model. Inner models with a measurable satisfy GCH, but V may not. The inner model program describes the canonical “core”. Whether V equals such a model is exactly the debate of §5. ⚠ 常见误区。某个大基数的内模型并不说明 V 就是那个模型。含可测基数的内模型满足 GCH，V 却未必。内模型纲领描述的是典范的“核心”，V 是否等于这样一个模型，正是 §5 争论的焦点。
§7

The HOD dichotomy and the HOD ConjectureHOD 二分定理与 HOD 猜想

Prerequisites前置: L22 · OD, HODOD 与 HOD L25 · extendible, supercompact可扩与超紧 L15 · Jensen coveringJensen 覆盖
One idea. Jensen's covering lemma says L is either very close to V or very far from it (0# exists). Woodin proved the same kind of fork for HOD, above an extendible cardinal. The HOD Conjecture says the “far” side never happens. Unlike L, there is no known “HOD-sharp” to make HOD far, which is why many expect the conjecture to be true. 核心思想。Jensen 覆盖引理说：L 要么离 V 很近，要么离 V 很远（0# 存在）。伍丁对 HOD 在可扩基数之上证明了同类的二分。HOD 猜想说“远离”的情形永远不会发生。与 L 不同，目前不知道有什么“HOD 的升号”能把 HOD 推远，这正是许多人预期该猜想为真的原因。

Toggle the two scenarios. Each tick is a cardinal above the extendible δ. Compare what HOD thinks of it with what V thinks. 切换两种情形。每个刻度是可扩基数 δ 之上的一个基数。对比 HOD 怎么看它、V 怎么看它。

HOD Dichotomy (Woodin) THEOREM. Suppose δ is an extendible cardinal. Then exactly one of the following holds:
(1) every singular cardinal γ > δ is singular in HOD and (γ⁺)^HOD = γ⁺;
(2) every regular cardinal γ > δ is ω-strongly measurable in HOD (in particular, measurable in HOD).
Goldberg (2020s) reduced the hypothesis from extendible to strongly compact.
HOD Hypothesis: there is a proper class of regular cardinals that are not ω-strongly measurable in HOD (so, with an extendible, case (1) holds). HOD Conjecture (Woodin) OPEN: the HOD Hypothesis is provable in ZFC + “there is an extendible cardinal”. Woodin showed that the Ultimate-L Conjecture implies (a slightly weaker form of) the HOD Conjecture. HOD 二分定理（伍丁） 定理。设 δ 是可扩基数。则下列两者恰有一个成立：
(1) 每个奇异基数 γ > δ 在 HOD 中也是奇异的，并且 (γ⁺)^HOD = γ⁺；
(2) 每个正则基数 γ > δ 在 HOD 中都是 ω-强可测的（特别地，在 HOD 中可测）。
Goldberg（2020 年代）把假设从可扩基数减弱到了强紧基数。
HOD 假说：存在真类多个在 HOD 中不是 ω-强可测的正则基数（于是在有可扩基数时，情形 (1) 成立）。HOD 猜想（伍丁） 未解决：HOD 假说在 ZFC + “存在可扩基数” 中可证。伍丁证明了：终极 L 猜想推出 HOD 猜想（的一个稍弱的形式）。
Why people care / 动机. HOD is a canonical, definable inner model of ZFC that exists no matter what. If it is close to V, then V has an L-like core that inherits all large cardinals, which is good news for Ultimate L. If it is far, there is a whole new realm of large cardinal structure “beyond HOD”. Recent work (Aguilera–Bagaria–Lücke 2024; Aguilera–Bagaria–Goldberg–Lücke 2025) introduced exacting and ultraexacting cardinals. They are consistent with ZFC relative to rank-into-rank axioms (exacting cardinals lie strictly between I3 and I2 in strength; ultraexacting ones are equiconsistent with I0), and they imply V ≠ HOD. An exacting cardinal above an extendible forces case (2), so if ZFC + “an exacting cardinal above an extendible” is consistent, the HOD Conjecture is false. Whether that combination is consistent is OPEN. 动机。HOD 是一个无论如何都存在的、可定义的、典范的 ZFC 内模型。如果它接近 V，那么 V 就有一个继承全部大基数的“像 L 的核心”，这对终极 L 是好消息；如果它远离 V，就会出现一整片“超越 HOD”的大基数新天地。最近的工作（Aguilera–Bagaria–Lücke 2024；Aguilera–Bagaria–Goldberg–Lücke 2025）引入了严格（exacting）基数与超严格（ultraexacting）基数，它们相对于秩到秩（rank-into-rank）公理与 ZFC 一致（严格基数的强度严格介于 I3 与 I2 之间；超严格基数与 I0 等一致），并推出 V ≠ HOD。可扩基数之上的严格基数会迫使情形 (2) 成立，所以若 ZFC + “可扩基数之上有严格基数” 一致，HOD 猜想就是错的。这种组合是否一致，未解决。
⚠ Common pitfall. The dichotomy is a theorem; the conjecture (that case (2) cannot happen, provably) is open. Also, V ≠ HOD by itself is harmless and easily forced (add a Cohen real). The dichotomy is about something much more drastic: whether HOD gets the cardinal structure above δ right. ⚠ 常见误区。二分是定理；猜想（情形 (2) 可证地不会发生）尚未解决。另外，V ≠ HOD 本身无伤大雅，很容易力迫得到（添加一个科恩实数即可）。二分定理关心的是严重得多的事：HOD 能否正确地把握 δ 之上的基数结构。

Beyond choice: Reinhardt and Berkeley cardinals超越选择公理：Reinhardt 基数与 Berkeley 基数

Prerequisites前置: L25 · Kunen inconsistency, I0–I3库能不相容性、I0–I3 L10 · Axiom of Choice选择公理 §7
One idea. Kunen (1971) showed that with AC there is no nontrivial elementary j : V → V. The proof uses choice. Without AC, nobody has refuted such embeddings, and they sit at the very top of the large-cardinal hierarchy, above the wall that AC builds. Whether they are consistent is linked to the HOD Conjecture. 核心思想。库能（1971）证明：在 AC 下不存在非平凡的初等嵌入 j : V → V。证明用到了选择公理。没有 AC 时，还没有人推翻这类嵌入，它们位于大基数层级的最顶端，在 AC 筑起的那堵墙之上。它们是否一致，和 HOD 猜想紧密相连。

Flip the AC switch to see which part of the ladder survives. Click a rung for its status. 拨动 AC 开关，看梯子的哪一段能幸存。点击一级阶梯查看其状态。

Definitions (in ZF). κ is Reinhardt if κ is the critical point of a nontrivial elementary j : V → V (j a class, with V satisfying ZF in the language with j). δ is Berkeley if for every transitive set M with δ ∈ M and every η < δ there is an elementary j : M → M with η < crit(j) < δ.
Kunen 1971 THEOREM: ZFC proves there is no Reinhardt cardinal; in fact no nontrivial j : Vλ+2 → Vλ+2.
Consistency of ZF + a Reinhardt cardinal OPEN. Known: assuming DC, a Reinhardt cardinal yields the consistency of ZFC + I0 (Goldberg). Schlutzenberg showed that ZF + a nontrivial j : Vλ+2 → Vλ+2 is consistent relative to ZFC + I0. A January 2026 preprint (McCallum, arXiv:2601.10231) claimed to refute Reinhardt cardinals in ZF; the author withdrew it on 23 August 2026 after an error was identified in its final section. (An earlier 2017 claim by the same author was withdrawn in January 2018.)
Link to HOD THEOREM (Woodin): (ZF) assume the HOD Conjecture is true. If δ is extendible, then for every λ > δ there is no nontrivial j : Vλ+2 → Vλ+2. Bagaria–Koellner–Woodin (2019) explain why, if Berkeley cardinals are consistent, the (weak) Ultimate-L Conjecture must fail. 定义（在 ZF 中）。若 κ 是某个非平凡初等嵌入 j : V → V 的临界点（j 为类，V 在含 j 的语言中满足 ZF），则称 κ 为 Reinhardt 基数。若对每个满足 δ ∈ M 的传递集 M 及每个 η < δ，都存在初等嵌入 j : M → M 使 η < crit(j) < δ，则称 δ 为 Berkeley 基数。
库能 1971 定理：ZFC 证明不存在 Reinhardt 基数；事实上不存在非平凡的 j : Vλ+2 → Vλ+2。
ZF + Reinhardt 基数的一致性 未解决。已知：在 DC 下，Reinhardt 基数给出 ZFC + I0 的一致性（Goldberg）。Schlutzenberg 证明了 ZF + 非平凡 j : Vλ+2 → Vλ+2 相对于 ZFC + I0 一致。2026 年 1 月有一篇预印本（McCallum，arXiv:2601.10231）声称在 ZF 中否证了 Reinhardt 基数；由于其最后一节被发现有错误，作者已于 2026 年 8 月 23 日撤稿。（同一作者 2017 年的类似声称已于 2018 年 1 月撤回。）
与 HOD 的联系 定理（伍丁）：（在 ZF 中）假设 HOD 猜想为真。若 δ 是可扩基数，则对每个 λ > δ 都不存在非平凡的 j : Vλ+2 → Vλ+2。Bagaria–Koellner–Woodin（2019）说明了：如果 Berkeley 基数是一致的，那么（弱）终极 L 猜想必定不成立。
Why people care / 动机. Two possibilities, and either is dramatic. If choiceless cardinals are inconsistent, the large cardinal hierarchy has a natural ceiling, and the inconsistency proof would probably say something deep about HOD (and support the HOD and Ultimate-L Conjectures). If they are consistent, then AC is what keeps the universe below them, and V is “far from HOD”-style structure is real. The question tests whether our large-cardinal intuitions are trustworthy all the way up. 动机。两种可能，无论哪种都很惊人。如果无选择大基数不一致，大基数层级就有了天然的天花板，而这个不一致性证明多半会揭示关于 HOD 的深刻事实（并支持 HOD 猜想与终极 L 猜想）。如果它们一致，那么正是 AC 把宇宙挡在它们之下，“V 远离 HOD”式的结构也就是真实存在的。这个问题检验的是：我们对大基数的直觉，能不能一路信到顶。
⚠ Common pitfall. “Reinhardt cardinals are inconsistent” is a theorem of ZFC, not of ZF. When people say the consistency of Reinhardt cardinals is open, they mean over ZF. Also beware of claimed refutations: there have been at least two (2017, 2026), both withdrawn. ⚠ 常见误区。“Reinhardt 基数不一致”是 ZFC 的定理，而不是 ZF 的定理。说 Reinhardt 基数的一致性尚未解决，指的是在 ZF 之上。还要警惕声称的否证：至少有过两次（2017、2026），都已撤回。

Cardinal arithmetic: Easton, SCH, and Shelah's ℵω bound基数算术：Easton、SCH 与 Shelah 的 ℵω 上界

Prerequisites前置: L9 · cofinality, König共尾性、König 定理 L16 · forcing 2^κ力迫 2^κ L24 · measurables, Prikry可测基数、Prikry
One idea. At regular cardinals, ZFC says almost nothing about 2^κ (Easton). At singular cardinals it says a lot, and the gap between what is proved and what is known to be consistent is one of the cleanest open problems in set theory. 核心思想。在正则基数处，ZFC 对 2^κ 几乎什么也没说（Easton）。在奇异基数处它却说了很多；“已证明的”与“已知一致的”之间的缺口，是集合论中最干净利落的未解问题之一。

Part A (regular). Pick a value for 2^ℵ₀. The checker applies König's theorem cf(2^κ) > κ. A 部分（正则）。给 2^ℵ₀ 选一个值。检查器会应用 König 定理 cf(2^κ) > κ。

Part B (singular). Assume ℵω is a strong limit (2^ℵₙ < ℵω for all n). Click a spot on the aleph line to propose a value of 2^ℵω. B 部分（奇异）。设 ℵω 是强极限基数（对一切 n，2^ℵₙ < ℵω）。点击阿列夫数轴上的一个位置，为 2^ℵω 提一个值。

consistent (relative to large cardinals if ≠ ℵω+1)一致（若 ≠ ℵω+1 则相对于大基数） open未解决 impossible (ZFC theorem)不可能（ZFC 定理）
Easton 1970 THEOREM. Over GCH, for any class function F on regular cardinals with F monotone and cf F(κ) > κ, some cardinal-preserving forcing makes 2^κ = F(κ) for all regular κ.
SCH: for every singular strong limit κ, 2^κ = κ⁺. Silver 1974 THEOREM: if κ is singular of uncountable cofinality and 2^λ = λ⁺ for all λ < κ, then 2^κ = κ⁺. Solovay 1974: SCH holds above a strongly compact cardinal. Gitik 1989/1991 THEOREM: ZFC + ¬SCH is equiconsistent with a measurable κ of Mitchell order o(κ) = κ⁺⁺.
Shelah (pcf theory, 1990s) THEOREM: if ℵω is a strong limit, then 2^ℵω < ℵω₄. Magidor 1977, Shelah, Gitik–Magidor 1992: for every countable α ≥ 1, “ℵω strong limit and 2^ℵω = ℵω+α+1” is consistent relative to large cardinals.
Open problem OPEN: if ℵω is a strong limit, must 2^ℵω < ℵω₁? Equivalently: can pcf{ℵₙ : n < ω} be uncountable? Shelah conjectures |pcf(A)| = |A| for progressive A, which would give the ω₁ bound. Easton 1970 定理。在 GCH 之上，对正则基数上任意满足单调且 cf F(κ) > κ 的类函数 F，存在保持基数的力迫，使得对所有正则 κ 都有 2^κ = F(κ)。
奇异基数假设（SCH）：对每个奇异强极限基数 κ，2^κ = κ⁺。Silver 1974 定理：若 κ 是共尾性不可数的奇异基数，且对所有 λ < κ 有 2^λ = λ⁺，则 2^κ = κ⁺。Solovay 1974：SCH 在强紧基数之上成立。Gitik 1989/1991 定理：ZFC + ¬SCH 与“存在 Mitchell 序 o(κ) = κ⁺⁺ 的可测基数 κ”等一致。
Shelah（可能共尾理论，即 pcf 理论，1990 年代） 定理：若 ℵω 是强极限，则 2^ℵω < ℵω₄。Magidor 1977、Shelah、Gitik–Magidor 1992：对每个可数序数 α ≥ 1，“ℵω 是强极限且 2^ℵω = ℵω+α+1” 相对于大基数一致。
未解决问题 未解决：若 ℵω 是强极限，是否必有 2^ℵω < ℵω₁？等价地：pcf{ℵₙ : n < ω} 能否不可数？Shelah 猜想对“渐进的”A 有 |pcf(A)| = |A|，由此即得 ω₁ 这个上界。
Why people care / 动机. Shelah's theorem was a shock: after Cohen and Easton, cardinal exponentiation looked hopelessly independent, yet pcf theory finds ZFC structure exactly at singular cardinals. It recasts the problem as the theory of cofinalities of reduced products ∏ A / D, with applications in algebra, topology and model theory. The ω₁-versus-ω₄ gap is the cleanest test of how much of this structure ZFC really controls. 动机。Shelah 的定理令人震惊：在科恩和 Easton 之后，基数幂看起来已毫无希望地处处独立，可 pcf 理论偏偏在奇异基数处找到了 ZFC 能证明的结构。它把问题改写成约化积 ∏ A / D 的共尾性理论，在代数、拓扑和模型论中都有应用。ω₁ 与 ω₄ 之间的缺口，正是检验 ZFC 到底掌控多少这种结构的最清晰试金石。
⚠ Common pitfall. The hypothesis “ℵω is a strong limit” matters. Without it, 2^ℵω ≥ 2^ℵ₀ can be made as large as you like by Easton forcing at ℵ₀, and the ω₄ bound says nothing. Also, the subscript is ω₄ (the fourth uncountable ordinal), not ω + 4: the bound allows 2^ℵω to sit above uncountably many alephs. ⚠ 常见误区。“ℵω 是强极限”这个假设很关键。没有它，用 Easton 力迫在 ℵ₀ 处可以让 2^ℵω ≥ 2^ℵ₀ 要多大有多大，ω₄ 上界也就无从谈起。另外，下标是 ω₄（第四个不可数序数），不是 ω + 4：这个上界允许 2^ℵω 高出不可数多个阿列夫。

Combinatorics of the continuum: Cichoń's diagram连续统的组合学：Cichoń 图

Prerequisites前置: L16 · forcing, ccc力迫与 ccc L19 · meager, null sets贫集与零测集
One idea. When CH fails, many natural “sizes” between ℵ₁ and 2^ℵ₀ can differ: how many null sets you need to cover ℝ, how small a non-meager set can be, and so on. ZFC proves a fixed web of inequalities between them (the arrows), and, remarkably, nothing more. 核心思想。当 CH 不成立时，介于 ℵ₁ 与 2^ℵ₀ 之间的许多自然“尺寸”可以各不相同：需要多少个零测集才能覆盖 ℝ，非贫集最小能有多小，等等。ZFC 证明了它们之间一张固定的不等式网（图中的箭头），而且值得注意的是，除此之外再无其他。

Why people care / 动机. On the universe view, §4 versus §5 is a genuine contest with one right answer, and evidence (fruitfulness, coherence, canonicity) is how we find it. ⚠ Common pitfall. Neither view is a theorem, and neither is “refuted by Gödel” or “proved by Cohen”. The incompleteness theorems and forcing are neutral data that both views must explain. ⚠ 常见误区。两种观点都不是定理，也谈不上“被哥德尔推翻”或“被科恩证明”。不完全性定理和力迫是中立的事实，两种观点都必须给出解释。
